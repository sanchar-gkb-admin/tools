Achievement / Performance — pillar lock for part-payment

A ₹4999 student who has only part-paid sees a teaser scorecard. The Achievement screen shows their pillar performance profile — Aptitude · Attitude · Communication · Execution + the overall AACE score. That profile is the paid deliverable, so for an unsettled part-payment student it must be withheld — while their completion (tasks done) stays visible.

The change: before the fix this screen leaked the real pillar percentages to a locked student (one tab over from the locked scorecard). After the fix, the pillars are masked with an unlock CTA; completion is untouched. A fully-paid / settled student is unchanged.
