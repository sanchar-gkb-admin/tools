Authoring 2A's pre & post challenges

The challenge CRUD is scan-shaped: one challenge per (program_key, tier), with price columns it no longer owns. 2A needs four challenges per stream, across five streams — 20 challenges, 140 tasks. This is what has to change so Sutirtha ji can author them.

Goutam, 27-Aug: “Sutirtha should be able to author this… that will unblock sutirtha and allow us to create these challenges and tasks with abilities mapping.”

A banner counts what the product declares against what exists, per stream.
