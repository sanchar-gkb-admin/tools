How we build
and sell things

We changed the way the whole system thinks about what we offer. Same products today — but from now on, launching something new is a decision, not a software project.

01Where we were

Everything about an offering lived in one box. Four different questions, one answer sheet.

Before
One box held all of it
What the student does What it costs Who's allowed to buy it How they paid How many tests they get How long they have Whether it's a free trial

Change one thing and you risked the others. Every new offering meant an engineer.

What that cost us
Wanted to bundle two things✕Code change
Wanted a school-only version✕Code change
Wanted to give something away✕Price it at zero — then we couldn't tell "we gifted this" from "this never had a price"
Asked "what can this student do?"✕Three different lists answered, and they disagreed

02The one decision that changed everything

Stop asking one box four questions. Give each question its own home.

After
1
What you getThe actual tasks and tests. Nothing else.
2

3
What you're allowed to doHow many sets, for how long, at what pace.
4
How you got itPaid, sponsored, waived, or free — about the person, not the product.
Why it matters

"What can this student do?"✓One answer, one place
A new price✓Typed in, not coded in

04What a recipe can say

Four building blocks. Everything we sell — and everything we might — is some arrangement of these.

BLOCK 01A part A scan, a test series, a live event, coaching. Add a new kind any time.
BLOCK 02An order All at once, or one after another — or "only once both of these are done".
BLOCK 03A repeat Eight sets a month. A task every week. Always with a finish line.
BLOCK 04A condition Only for a certain age, or only once a report is ready.

05Who we can sell to

The buyer is now something we write down, not something baked in. Adding one is a line in a list.

Families direct Schools Colleges Companies CSR programmes Government + whatever comes next

And each one can have its own task set, its own starting questions, and its own agreed price — without affecting anybody else.

06What it takes to launch, now

STEP 01Decide it What's in it, what it costs, who it's for.

STEP 03It appears On the brochure, priced, ready.
