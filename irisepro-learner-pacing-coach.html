Everything is configurable, nothing hard-coded

  • One source for every figure: Day X / 25 / days left come from the unit window written by LearnerWindow; done / 21 from the dashboard's existing $progress. The card, the strip, the catch-up plan, push, email and the parent note all read these. None keeps its own copy.
  • Proposed config keys (values drawn here are proposals): pacing.enabled · pacing.time_slots (6:30 / 16:30 / 19:00 / 20:30) · pacing.on_pace_rule (floor(total × day ÷ duration)) · pacing.closing_soon_days (3) · pacing.catchup_max_per_day (≤ daily cap when on) · pacing.channels (push, email; whatsapp at launch) · pacing.parent_note.day_time (Sun 10:00) · pacing.streak.enabled.
  • All copy in en + kn as translation keys, including the prep line, the five card states, the streak lines, both catch-up plans and the parent note. Kannada is not drawn here; it needs native review before build.
  • Existing emails stay (onboarding 1/3/5, completion 7/18/23/24, window 50/80/95%). Pacing reminders join them under the same MailGate brake (SendWindowNudge.php:43).
  • Voice: YASHHO no-fear. No red, no ticking countdown, always one next step. Learners see "challenge", never "task".

Open facts (UNVERIFIED)

  • Timer minutes are from the seed snapshot (prod-catalog-mapping.tsv: 30-120 min for this programme). Prod values were not read. The timer is the submission window, not how long the work takes, so "10 minutes" is the daily habit, not a challenge's length.
  • Whether any learner push is sent today (Firebase SW + device_token exist; no sender was checked).
  • The "F8112" no-MX case comes from the brief and is not in the repo.
