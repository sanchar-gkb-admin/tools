This reverses yesterday's IAS decision — the spec must change

Yesterday IAS was to be one stream with the exam captured at enrolment as exam_target. It is now its own stream with its own 12 offer rows and 6 engines, exactly as the updated brochure has it. Spec §13.6 in PR #1641 states the old decision and is now wrong. exam_target is no longer needed for this purpose.

What "its own stream" costs, stated plainly

IAS and KAS carry the same engines at the same prices, so the two streams hold duplicate product rows. That is a deliberate trade: separate streams give IAS its own tab position, URL, benchmarks and page — at the price of changing a shared price in two places. Worth a guard test asserting the two stay in step, so a drift is caught rather than discovered by a buyer.

2C still needs five products

neet_jee has engine 1 with a single product (₹5,999). Engines 2, 3 and 4 are empty. Its siblings each carry 4 engines and 8 products. Five products and their prices are the gap before 2C can have a URL.

Institutions has nothing to sell against

Tab 4 is five brochure pages with no catalogue rows at all. It can ship as content — an enquiry-and-webinar tab with no prices — but it cannot render an Engines & Price band until B2B products exist. Decide which, because it changes whether the tab has a Success Scan CTA or a Talk-to-us CTA.

Two faults, not one

A 4:5 poster cannot fill a 9:19.5 screen without losing ~40% of its width — and what sits in that 40% is the Minister's face and the quote. Separately, the six cards are three different aspect ratios, so they visibly jump size as the carousel rotates.

The fix applied above

Every card is composited onto one 9:16 stage painted --blue-deep #021F5E, then rendered object-fit:contain. Nothing crops, nothing jumps, and the band below the card becomes the natural home for the dots and the CTA rail. Ships today with no new artwork.

E Spec for mobile-native re-exports

The staging above is correct and buildable now. Re-cutting the artwork for a tall screen is better, because the card then uses the whole display instead of ~72% of it.

DeliverableSpec
Mobile master1080 × 1920 (9:16). Portrait crop of the subject, quote stacked beneath, HOLE wordmark large. This is the one that matters — most traffic is phones.
Safe areaKeep all text inside the central 1080 × 1500. The top ~220px sits under the logo and notch; the bottom ~200px under dots and the CTA rail.
Desktop masterKeep the existing 4:5 — it is right for a three-up desktop band.
UniformityAll six identical in each set. One aspect, one margin, one type scale. Today card 3 is a third ratio for no reason.

FormatWebP or AVIF with JPEG fallback. Card 1 preloaded — it is the LCP element.

F Build notes for full-screen carousels

Use 100dvh, never 100vh, on mobile

100vh on iOS Safari measures the viewport without the address bar, so a full-screen slide is taller than the screen and the controls sit below the fold until the user scrolls. 100dvh tracks the bar as it collapses. Keep 100vh as a fallback line above it for old browsers.

Controls belong in the safe area

Dots and the CTA rail need padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom) or the home indicator sits on top of them on every notched iPhone.

Auto-scroll needs a pause and a stop

Pause on touch, pause on prefers-reduced-motion, and stop at card 6. Card 6 is where the pun resolves and the founder appears — a loop that carries a reader past it spends the whole build for nothing.

The first card must not be lazy

Card 1 is the LCP element. Preload it and let cards 2–6 lazy-load, or the largest paint on the most important page waits on a decode.

Swipe must not fight the page scroll

Horizontal swipe on a full-height slide competes with vertical scroll. Use CSS scroll-snap on a horizontal track with touch-action: pan-y pinch-zoom so a downward drag still scrolls the page.

Two copy conflicts to settle

The updated brochure still reads Scan · Show · Strengthen · Succeed, in two places. The locked tagline is SCAN · RECOGNISE · ACTIVATE · REALISE. Also new: a 24 Abilities for Marks cluster — a marks subset of the 64 that did not exist in v29 and needs a home in the ability model.
