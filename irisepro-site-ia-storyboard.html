C ⛔ Three conflicts to resolve before this is built

Found by diffing the updated brochure against the catalogue and against decisions taken 20-Aug.

ConflictDetailNeeds
IAS is duplicated The update gives IAS its own stream — sections 24–26, 12 offer rows, 6 engines, a full copy of KAS. But the decision on 20-Aug was one stream, IAS captured at enrolment as exam_target, same engines and prices. Building it as a stream creates 12 duplicate product rows and two price masters for identical products. Goutam vs KMM — one call
NEET-JEE 2X has vanished Zero occurrences of “2X” or “NEET-JEE” in the updated file. It exists in the catalogue as neet_jee (Group 2C) and was confirmed on 20-Aug as needing all engines. The redesign has no tab slot for it. Does 2C ship? Where?
Institutions is not in the catalogue Tab 4 is 5 new pages — Whole-Institution, Teacher Success Scan, Scientific Hiring, Future-Readiness, Political Leadership. The catalogue has 7 streams and no institutional products. Nothing to price or sell against yet. B2B catalogue, or brochure-only for now
What the update got right

Group 3 is renamed to KAS · IAS · State Civil Services — exactly the fix flagged on 20-Aug, since naming a group after its only member breaks the moment a second joins. Three new framework pages and six All-India Benchmarks pages were also added; 15 sections → 32.

Worth a legal read before it is the first thing every visitor sees

All six cards are built on a sitting Finance Minister's photograph and quotes. The quotes are sourced to a published interview, which helps. The photographs and the framing — “who better than India's Finance Minister…” — read as endorsement of a paid product. Personality rights and implied endorsement are the exposure. A decision to take knowingly, not by default.

The carousel is a section type, not a hard-coded band

Six cards today, four next month, a different campaign at Diwali. If the carousel is a carousel_cards instance, all of that is uploading images and reordering rows. If it is hand-built markup, every change is a deploy — which is the exact problem this rebuild exists to end.

Tabs must be URLs

A JS-only tab toggle gives all four tabs one URL: no deep link from an ad, no separate SEO, no analytics per group, and the back button skips the whole section. /regular, /neet-jee, /civil-services, /institutions — rendered as tabs, addressed as pages.
