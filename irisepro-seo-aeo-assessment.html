Where we stand on SEO and AEO

The technical foundations are in better shape than most sites at this stage. The problem is not the plumbing — the brand has no index presence yet, and there is nothing on the site for a search engine to rank beyond the product pages themselves.

The short version

Structured data, canonicals and the domain migration are all done properly. Three things hold us back: the sitemap declares fewer than half our pages, the old domain still owns the brand's search equity, and there is no content layer — so there is nothing to rank for anything except our own name.

Read this before acting on it. I verified what the site serves — headers, redirects, markup, sitemap — directly against live. I cannot see our actual rankings: the search tooling available to me indexes US results, our market is India, and I have no Search Console access. Everything about position is inference, not a rank report. Step 01 of the plan exists to close exactly that gap.

The gaps, in the order they cost us

01

The old domain still owns our search equity

Searching our own brand and proposition returns irisepro.in, describing the old offering. yashho.com does not appear at all. The 301 is correct, so this is reprocessing lag — but lag nobody has asked Google to resolve, because the new domain has never been submitted.

Fix: Verify yashho.com in Search Console, submit the sitemap, run change-of-address from the irisepro.in property. Hours of work, then weeks of waiting. Nothing else here matters until this is done.

02

The sitemap hides more than half the site

It declares three URLs — home, /godparent, /institutional. Missing: /kas/en, /kas/kn, /policies. The Kannada page is the single most differentiated asset we have and no crawler is being told it exists.

Fix: Add them, and declare the EN/KN pair as hreflang alternates so they read as one page in two languages, not duplicates. Half a day.

03

Review markup a visitor cannot see

The page claims 4.6 from 55 reviews in structured data, but that rating appears nowhere on the page. Google requires review markup to reflect what users can actually see. This is a documented cause of rich-result suppression and manual actions — and a manual action would undo everything else on this list.

Fix: Render the rating and reviews so the markup is true, or remove aggregateRating and review. Do not leave it as it is.

04

Nothing to rank except the product

Seven pages, all selling. Search engines rank pages against questions, and we have published no answers — nothing on how ability assessment works, what the 64 abilities are, what a parent should look for, how this differs from an aptitude test.

Fix: A genuine content layer. This is the long pole — see the sequence below.

05

Legacy URLs redirect into a fragment

/career-rise resolves to yashho.com/#offerings. Crawlers discard the fragment, so this is a redirect to the homepage. Many-to-one redirects onto the homepage are commonly treated as soft-404s and pass little of the old page's value.

Fix: Point each retired URL at the closest real page, or give offerings its own addressable page.

06

No AEO surface for answer engines

The FAQ schema is genuinely good and is our strongest AEO asset. But there is no llms.txt, and more importantly, answer engines cite what multiple independent sources corroborate. We are one self-describing source with an inconsistent identity across profiles.

Fix: Publish llms.txt, finish the sameAs cleanup, pursue third-party corroboration.

Can we actually be #1–#3?

It depends entirely on which query — and the honest answer differs sharply between the two kinds.

Yes — and it should already be true

Our own name and vocabulary

“YASHHO”, “Success Scan”, “Yashodarshi”, “AACE64”, “Kannadiga-invented”. Nobody competes for these — we invented them. We are not #1 today only because the new domain has not been indexed under them.

Gaps 01 and 02 fix it. Weeks, not months, and no new content required.

Not without a content programme

Category terms parents type

“career guidance for students”, “aptitude test for my child”, “NEET or JEE — which suits my child”. Contested by sites with years of published material and inbound links.

Seven selling pages cannot win these at any budget. A 6–12 month programme of publishing and earning citations. No configuration change substitutes for it.

The useful ground is between them: long-tail questions with real intent and thin competition — “how do I know which stream suits my child”, “what is an ability assessment”, “PU1 vs PU2 career readiness” — and the entire Kannada-language equivalent set, where competition is close to nil and we hold a native asset nobody else has. Winning several hundred of those beats losing one head term, and it is the same work that makes us citable by answer engines.

The sequence

Ordered by dependency, not ambition. Each step is worth less if the one above it has not happened.

  1. This week · hours

    Verify yashho.com in Search Console, submit the sitemap

    Also run change-of-address from the irisepro.in property. Until this exists we are guessing about position — this document included. The single highest-value hour available.

  2. This week · half a day

    Complete the sitemap and add hreflang

    Add /kas/en, /kas/kn, /policies. Declare the EN/KN pair as alternates so Kannada reads as a language variant, not a duplicate.

  3. This week · one hour

    Resolve the review markup

    Show the rating on the page, or take it out of the schema. An unverifiable claim in structured data risks a penalty costing more than the rich result earns.

  4. Next · a day

    Fix legacy redirects, publish llms.txt

    Retired URLs point at real pages rather than a homepage fragment. llms.txt gives answer engines a clean statement of what we are and which pages matter.

  5. Ongoing · the real work

    Publish answers, in both languages

    One substantial piece a week against a real parent question, English and Kannada, each carrying FAQ schema — the mechanism already built and working. This is what makes us rankable on long-tail search and quotable by answer engines; those are now largely the same job.

  6. Ongoing · outside the codebase

    Earn third-party corroboration

    Answer engines repeat what several independent sources agree on. Press, an accurate Wikidata entry, institutional partner pages, directory listings. A site describing itself is one source; corroboration is what turns us into a cited answer.

What I could not check. Keyword positions on google.co.in; Search Console impressions, clicks and coverage; backlink profile; Core Web Vitals field data. All need account access — step 01 unlocks them.
