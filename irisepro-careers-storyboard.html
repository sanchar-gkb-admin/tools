01 · Careers page — /careers

Reached from Careers in the header menu and footer. Common hiring poster on top, then 5 jobs, then a separate Partner with us block for YSP.

Every card, pay figure and form link comes from one roles file; the cards, role pages, Google job listing data and FAQ all read it. No pay figure is typed into a page.

02 · Role page — /careers/regional-sales-manager

The role's portrait poster, key facts in big type, then details. Apply stays fixed at the bottom while scrolling.

One "Before you apply" list for every role (the source copy varied by role). Region is chosen in the form, not on the page.

03 · Head-office role — same layout for all 4 salaried roles

GM and the 3 managers use the same page, each with its own poster, facts and form. Shown: Field Sales Network and GM.

Open: the GM poster says "Preferred age 35–40" and adds "preferably school/college admissions or EdTech" and "experience leading sales teams". The GM text document has none of these. Shown with "?" until you decide.

04 · Partner page — /careers/yashho-success-partner

Same layout, but a "Commission only — no fixed salary" banner sits above everything, and the earnings note is never hidden.

05 · Tap Apply → "Before you open the form"

A short checklist so nobody gets stuck halfway through the Google Form. One tap continues.

06 · The Google Form opens in a new tab

Outside our site. The careers tab stays open, so the applicant can switch back to check the requirements.

Every Apply link opens in a new tab. All 6 forms ask for a Google sign-in (checked 18 Nov). This mock is only a sketch of the form. Surya owns the real forms.

07 · Tap a poster → full screen

Posters carry small text, so a tap opens them full screen. Close with ✕ or swipe down.

08 · Desktop

Same content and order. Desktop uses the landscape posters, puts the 5 jobs in a grid, and shows the checklist as a centred box.
