Scorecard component architecture

One panel library, two surfaces. Every product is a list of panel names and nothing else. Change the configuration on the right and the card rebuilds — no template is edited, no code is added.
The scan already works this way. 1A/1B/1C are not three cards — they are one engine template branching on a single value, tier (5 / 15 / 64). The MCQ card extends that grammar rather than starting a second one.

THE FINDING — the scan and the MCQ share one ability vocabulary, and it is 15 = 11 + 4. The scan's §4 spotlights 15 abilities for KAS. The MCQ papers carry 11. Ten of the eleven match the scan's names exactly; the eleventh is one ability under two surface names (“Structure / Information Organisation” on the paper, “Structure (Written)” on the scan). The four the scan has and the papers do not are Discipline, Time-Management, Efficiency, Accountability — exactly the four exam-behavioural abilities we score from observed behaviour. So the split was designed in from the start: eleven measured from answers, four from behaviour, fifteen on the card.

Two consequences. First, the scan_link panel is sound — the two instruments can be put side by side on the same row, because they name the same thing. Second, Sutirtha ji's note (“benchmark for 15 abilities are missing sir, as well as the Gap”) was not a request for something new: the scan card has carried student-vs-developmental-vs-champion-vs-competitive plus three gap columns for all fifteen since day one. The MCQ card was missing a panel that already exists.

Provenance — scorecard-engine/design/scorecard-full.html:1437 (the tier branch), design/samples/sample-kas-{999,1999}.json and sample-kas.json (the per-tier section sets), tests/test_render_contract.py (the 15-row §4 and 15-ability §1-F contract). Panel keys in the library above are the engine's own JSON keys.

The rule that matters: a panel whose data contract is unmet is rendered as visibly unavailable, with the reason — never silently dropped. Otherwise a broken import looks exactly like a product that does not include that panel, and we would ship a card missing its most useful section without noticing.
