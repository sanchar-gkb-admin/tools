When something is wrong

Most problems are a blurred photo or a typo. They are fixed by resubmitting the form, not by sending documents on WhatsApp.

1Something to fix

Mohan's bank account is in his father's name. The desk marks "Problem" with a note and sends the standard message.

YASHHO Onboarding
Namaskara Mohan. We couldn't verify your bank details because the account is not in your name. We can only pay into an account in your own name. Please submit the form again here: forms.gle/…

2Not taken forward

If a candidate can't be verified or isn't right for the role, the row is marked Rejected with a reason. Their documents are deleted after 90 days.

When someone leaves

Access stops the same day. Families move to another YSP. Money already earned is paid. Documents are kept only as long as tax rules require.

1Switching someone off

The RSM records that Imran is leaving. The desk switches him off in the CRM, which ends his access on every phone at once.

YASHHO CRM · People · Leavers

Switch off: Imran (KA-R11-YSP04)

286 families moved to Deepa by Suresh · 2 paid orders keep Imran's commission

Drive folder → 4 Exited · final payout on 5 Jan with refund take-backs

Switch off now

How long exited YSPs' documents are kept is for Finance and legal to set.
