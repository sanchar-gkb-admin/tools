The school buys seats.The money arrives by invoice.

Everything exclusive to the institutional funnel, screen by screen: the agreement and its roster, the negotiated offer and per-line seats, the tax invoice and its three instalments, recorded collections with TDS, commission on what is collected, seats that start only when money is realised, the weekly delivery batch, the scan/Godparent gates, and the grace → pause → termination ladder. A school never checks out and never logs in; its money moves on its own path and still lands in the one ledger.

The seam: two funnels, one ledger

A family pays by gateway. A school is invoiced. The two never touch, and both feed the same ledger and the same commission calculator.

A family buying its own scan

Checkout, Razorpay order, webhook amount gate, payments and offer_redemptions, commission keyed payment:{rzp}:…. The direct scan is 7% YSP.

A school buying seats

Agreement, offer line, invoice, recorded collection by NEFT/RTGS/UPI/cheque, commission keyed collection:{id}:…. Every institutional line is 5% YSP / 0.5% RSM.

The invariant that decides every screen below

An institution is a tag on a sale and a partner in reporting — never an owner of a family, never a login, never a payee. A school's students and teachers are the institution's people, never crm_families. No institutional line is ever served to the storefront or priced through a /go/{slug} link.

The Acharya distinction, in one line

Mission Acharya is the scan product for teachers, listed under the academic products catalogue. It is not the package's scan and it has no Godparent — the annual package's scan and Godparent mentoring are students-only. The classroom-facing Academy courses are offline and partner-delivered.

One catalogue, dated versions, no per-school SKU

Products live in the one sellable_products catalogue under governed, dated versions. The catalogue says what may be sold; the school's offer says at what price and how many seats. Nothing institutional is served to the storefront.

Screen by screen

The order a school actually goes through. Each screen names its actor, what it writes, and the rule that binds it.

S1YSPThe institution record
Sees
Name, contact, coordinator, status — and now the GST identity a tax invoice needs: legal name, GSTIN, billing address, state code. Existing rows stay untouched (nullable columns).
Writes
institutions extension: code, kind (school / college / institute), region, gstin, state_code.
An institution is a tag and a partner in reporting — never an owner of a family, never a login. The list that will become the roster is filed under it.
S2YSPDraft the agreement
Sees
The institution, the signing YSP, the term dates, the signed document, and status draft → signed. One active agreement per institution; a new term is a new agreement.
Writes
institution_agreements: institution, signing YSP, term, signed_on, document_ref.
Signing is not a sale: it moves no money and starts no seat (R107.1). Approval is the RSM's, not the YSP's.
S3RSMApprove the agreement
Sees
Student strength, the agreement and its documents; approves to active. The designated YSP and RSM are stamped on the line here.
Writes
approved_at/approved_by; the roster attaches as the candidate list; the designated earners are fixed for every seat on the line.
The roster exists from approval whatever the invoice status — but a rosters place is only candidate. Money is what starts the seat.
S4RSMBuild the offer: one line per product, seats per line
Sees
1,000 package seats, 200 Acharya seats and 60 Academy seats can sit on one agreement — each line with its own negotiated price and its own cap_seats; offers.cap_total is the outer ceiling.
Writes
offer_items: product, negotiated price (net; stored GST-inclusive as R105.6(a) provides), cap_seats; the line closes independently at its cap.
No per-institution SKU. One school's price is never another's, and a closed line never falls back to the list price.
S5RSMThe instalment schedule
Sees
Three instalments per package seat: May, August, November 2027. Each row: sequence, due date, seats billed, net and gross.
Writes
institution_invoice_schedules: seq, due_on, seats_billed, gross_paise, status.
The schedule's nets must sum exactly to the line's negotiated net total, and its grosses to that × (1 + GST). A schedule row is invoiced once; cancelling one never silently re-splits the rest.
S6RSMRaise the instalment invoice
Sees
Series INV/{FY}/{seq} assigned under a row lock; both GSTINs, place of supply, HSN/SAC, CGST/SGST or IGST split; net, GST and gross frozen at issue; a rendered tax-invoice PDF.
Writes
institution_invoices: number, issued_on, due_on, gross_paise, gst_bp, net_paise, the split, place_of_supply_state, status.
A tax-invoice number is never reused or re-sequenced. GST liability follows the earlier of issue and receipt. An issued invoice is voided only before the first collection; after that the correction is a credit note. A number without a document is not a tax invoice — the PDF is slice A.
S7RSMRecord a collection
Sees
Date received, the bank credit, TDS withheld, the mode (NEFT/RTGS/UPI/cheque/cash), the UTR/cheque reference, a note. Partial payments are normal.
Writes
institution_collections: amount_gross_paise (bank credit + tds_paise), net_paise (de-GST'd at the invoice's frozen rate), recorder, confirmed_at.
The same (invoice, reference) cannot be recorded twice — a double entry of one bank credit is refused. Overpayment is refused, never absorbed. Rows are append-only; a correction is a credit note plus a new collection. The final collection absorbs the paise residual, so collections sum exactly to the invoice's frozen net.
S8SystemCommission accrues on the collected net
Sees
Two ledger lines per collection: the designated YSP's 5% and the designated RSM's 0.5% of the net settled.
Writes
partner_ledger_entries keyed collection:{id}:commission and collection:{id}:commission:network — idempotent, distinct from payment:{rzp}:….
TDS is part of the net the school settled, so it never cuts either earner's share. Sum of collections' commissions equals rate × the invoice's net when fully collected. The "paid on the 5th" payout run does not exist; this stops at accrual plus a manual admin payout.
S9SystemSeats unlock when money is realised
Sees
The seats a collection unlocks: settled money ÷ the instalment's per-seat price, floored; the remainder carries to the next collection.
Writes
Seat assignments against the line; the roster stays the candidate list.
A realised first instalment is the only thing that unlocks and starts a seat — not the upload, not the signature, not the invoice. A student's first task still makes it a sale and a target (R107.1), but does not start it.
S10RSMThe weekly delivery batch
Sees
Paid seats go out in batches on a configured cadence — Sunday by default. A batch takes the seats whose money was realised by its cut-off: typically 30–50, up to 1,000, whatever the payments support.
Writes
institution_batches: offer line, agreement, sequence, starts_on, seat count, status.
A batch never exceeds the line's cap and is never padded with money that has not arrived; unpaid seats wait for a later batch. Cadence, cut-off, typical size and ceiling are dated config, never code.
S11SystemThe combo's two gates — scan first, Godparent second
Sees
The package's scan starts on instalment 1; its Godparent starts on instalment 2. Each component carries its unlocking sequence as data (unlock_on_seq), never earlier than R109.2's day-0 / month-2 offsets.
Writes
Component gate data on the catalogue row; the component's start is the later of its offset and its gate being paid.
Scan is the precursor that gates Godparent — that is exactly why instalment 1 unlocks the scan and not Godparent. Instalments 2 and 3 bill the same seats and add none; instalment 3 unlocks no new component. The package never degrades silently to scan-only.
S12SystemThe window is fixed — and late money has a path
Sees
A seat's start and its 12-month anniversary never move. A late instalment keeps Godparent running through a 30–60 day grace window; unpaid at its end the package pauses; unpaid 120 days past due the line is terminated as not paid.
Writes
Grace/pause derived from due dates and collections; termination recorded once on the agreement line (ended) with the reason — never a credit note.
Services continue through the grace window so a late school sees no disruption. After it, Godparent stops and the scan already delivered stands. A later payment resumes Godparent but never moves the anniversary; collected money on a terminated line is not refunded.
S13System + RSMReminders, once per step
Sees
Offsets in days (e.g. −15, 0, +7, +30) from a dated config family; the invoice by email from the YASHHO billing desk to the coordinator; the nudge by WhatsApp from the designated RSM, with the YSP copied.
Writes
One row per (invoice, offset) in institution_invoice_reminders before sending, so a rerun never double-sends.
Logs and responses carry counts, post codes and invoice numbers — never a full phone number or email.
S14SystemAging — derived, never stored
Sees
Per invoice: outstanding = gross − collected, bucketed 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ days past due, plus not-yet-due.
Writes
Nothing. The buckets and the grace/pause/termination ladder read from the same outstanding figure; only a termination is recorded.
No stored total, no silent zero (R99.8). A school's page and Finance's view render from the rows.
S15FinanceConfirm the collection
Sees
Recorded collections not yet matched, and bank credits with no invoice reference (unmatched). Confirms a recorded collection against the credit and any TDS.
Writes
confirmed_at/confirmed_by; the ability is billing.confirm.
Recording is not reconciling. Money shown to the field is recorded; Finance distinguishes recorded, confirmed and unmatched. Recorded amounts are never edited (credit note instead).
S16Finance / GMThe institution statement
Sees
Invoices, collections and balance for one institution, rendered from the rows — never from a stored total.
Writes
Nothing; it is a view.
A school's people see their own statement; no school ever logs in to the CRM.
S17RSMCredit note — correction, not refund
Sees
Number, date, amount, reason, the invoice it corrects; the reversal debits each earner pro rata to the credited net.
Writes
institution_credit_notes; ledger keys credit_note:{id}:commission[:network]; the original rows stay.
Grounds are our own error only — a corrected seat count, a wrong or duplicate line, a service not delivered through our fault. A withholding, dispute or mid-term exit is never credited; KMM's no-refund policy stands.
S18YSPThe read-only line view
Sees
The line, its seats, its invoices, the money against them, and their own commission accrual.
Writes
Nothing. A YSP recording attempt is refused (D17, RSM-19).
The designated YSP earns on every seat of the line; the designated RSM earns the 0.5% network line. Neither is re-decided per collection.

The salient points — what is exclusive to institutions

If a reader remembers ten things about the institutional funnel, these are the ten.

1 · No school checkout, no school login

The school is invoiced; money arrives by bank. Nothing institutional reaches the gateway or the storefront.

2 · One ledger, two paths

Gateway and invoice paths write the same partner_ledger_entries through the same calculator; keys keep them apart.

3 · Net is the truth, GST is on top

Every KMM figure is net revenue; 18% GST is added. Leadership's GST-inclusive billings view is a display lens, never a target denominator.

4 · TDS is a settlement line

Bank credit + TDS = amount settled; commission is on the full net settled, TDS included.

5 · Seats are money

Settled money ÷ the per-seat first instalment, floored; the remainder carries. The roster is only the candidate list.

6 · Weekly batches, sized by what was paid

Sunday by default; typically 30–50, up to 1,000; never above the line's cap; a batch is never padded.

7 · The combo has two gates

Scan on instalment 1, Godparent on instalment 2 — scan is the precursor that gates Godparent. Acharya is a separate teachers' scan with no Godparent.

8 · The window never moves

A late payment never shifts a seat's start or the 12-month anniversary; grace 30–60 days, then pause, then termination at 120 days past due.

9 · Corrections are credit notes

Our error only, pro rata on both lines; a withholding, dispute or exit is never credited. No refund product.

10 · Late money is a ladder, not a cliff

Services continue through the grace window so the school sees no disruption; only then does Godparent pause, and only at 120 days does the line terminate.

The tests that matter most

The schedule sums to net × seats × (1 + GST), always. Invoice numbers are gap-free under concurrency. A frozen split ignores a later rate change. The same (invoice, reference) cannot be recorded twice. A partial payment earns on the amount received, and the collections together earn exactly rate × net. A TDS-bearing collection earns on the full net settled. A credit note reverses both lines and leaves the originals. A seat starts only on a realised first instalment, in the next batch; instalments 2–3 add no seat; Godparent never starts before instalment 2. A late instalment keeps Godparent running through grace, pauses it at the window's end and terminates at 120 days. A YSP recording is refused. No institutional line ever reaches the gateway or the storefront. No log line carries a full phone number or email.

The arithmetic on a 640-student May instalment: ₹32,00,000 net + ₹5,76,000 GST = ₹37,76,000 invoiced; YSP 5% = ₹1,60,000, RSM 0.5% = ₹16,000. RSM-19's unit test, which treated ₹32,00,000 as gross, is superseded by the exclusive-GST ruling.
