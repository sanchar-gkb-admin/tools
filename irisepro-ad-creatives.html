YASHHO — Empathy Copy + Storyboard

Written to the Empathy Copy Framework™, 12 lines, no line skipped. Then the visual arc built from the copy — because the emotional arc and the tonal arc have to be the same arc.

ONE HONEST ADAPTATION — LINE 10. The framework offers "Social Proof / Personal Proof". We do not yet have longitudinal outcome data, and the Message Master forbids inventing an individual result. So every copy below uses personal proof — what the founder observed as a serving KAS officer. That is true, unpurchasable, and stronger than a borrowed statistic.

LINE 8 — "you'll learn how to" becomes "you'll know". We are not teaching. We are showing. The verb has to be honest or the whole category blurs back into coaching.

TONE. Written to one person, as a message to a friend. No jargon, no poster voice, nothing clever. This is the correction to everything I sent before.

The narrative arc — and why the visual arc must match it

The 12 lines are not a list. They are a descent and a lift. If the visuals stay one tone throughout, the copy's emotional movement is flattened — which is exactly what was wrong with everything I made before.

I

Alone in the dark

Lines 1–4. Night, close, quiet, no product. The viewer must feel recognised before they will accept anything.

II

The turn

Lines 5–6. First light. One sentence of hope, held longer than feels comfortable.

III

The instrument

Lines 7–10. Clarity, evidence, the founder's face. Cool blue — clinical, calm, credible.

IV

Warmth

Lines 11–12. Full warm orange. The invitation is soft, and the last line is affection, not a CTA.

Production notes

Cast real parents, not actors. Frame 06 is three real faces saying one real sentence; the moment it is performed, the film dies.
Never show the marks. Not once. The parent supplies the number from memory, and theirs is always worse than anything we could print.
No music until Frame 06. The first 26 seconds carry room tone only. Silence in a feed is itself a pattern interrupt.
The girl in Frame 10 must be the girl in Frame 01. Same room, same desk, same lamp — lit differently. That rhyme is the film's only argument, and it is made without a word.
Cutdowns: 15s = Frames 01→03→07→11. 6s bumper = Frames 01→10.
