YASHHO — Ad Creative Storyboard

12 rendered variants · Hormozi value equation · Brunson hook–story–offer & false-belief demolition · empathy-led · 18 August 2026

Two brand rules constrain the frameworks, and both are deliberate.

ZERO FEAR. Hormozi's playbook leans on pain agitation. Naming a pain the parent already feels is empathy; manufacturing dread is not. Every ad below names an existing frustration and none predicts disaster.

NO OUTCOME GUARANTEE. No ad promises a mark, rank or admission. The proof offered is revelation — what nobody has seen — because that is what we can demonstrate today. Ad 12 turns that restraint into the differentiator.

THE TEST: if a coaching institute could run the ad with their logo on it, it is the wrong ad.

A · Empathy-led — name the pain they already carry

Start where the parent already is, in their own words, and withhold the product until the pain is named accurately. The trust comes from precision, not persuasion.
