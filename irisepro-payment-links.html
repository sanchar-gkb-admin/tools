01The link structure

There is exactly one enrolment form and one amount authority. The public URL carries an opaque plan slug, never the tier or the price — so a link cannot be tampered into a cheaper purchase.

https://yashho.com/enrollment_form?plan=<slug>&stream=<stream>[&lang=kn][&utm_*]

plan slug   ultimate-champion-64   → 1A · ASLI64 Master Scan  · ₹4,999
            success-champion-15    → 1B · ASLI15 Focus Scan   · ₹1,999
            success-champion-5     → 1C · ASLI5 Starter Scan  · ₹999

stream      marks_booster · board_booster · career_readiness · neet
            jee · kas · ias        (neet_jee is NOT purchasable — see 04)

lang=kn     KAS Kannada campaign arrivals (changes copy + the Back link only)
The amount is never taken from the browser. /api/order asks the Laravel catalogue for the authoritative amount for (tier, stream) via /api/checkout/resolve, and returns 503 rather than guess if the backend cannot answer. The price in plans.ts is display-only. GST is ON at 18%, so ₹4,999 charges ₹5,898.82.

04The gaps

neet_jee is advertised but cannot be bought. The catalogue lists neet_jee_1a as sellable + live at ₹5,999, but /api/checkout/resolve returns available:false for all three tiers — and echoes ₹4,999, not ₹5,999. So the catalogue and checkout disagree on both availability and price. Decision: fix the data and make it buyable at ₹5,999.
37 pre-order products have no payment path. They are marked sellable but fulfilment_state: preorder, and the URL contract cannot address them. Decision: take real pre-order payment — which requires the addressing change in 05 and creates a fulfilment/refund obligation that must be stated at checkout.
The brochure is not payment-linked. enrollment_form appears 0 times in brochure/page.tsx — every link is a filter (view/stream/engine/tier). A buyer can browse all 59 products and cannot buy any of them from that page.

05Proposed: one addressing authority

Keep the single amount authority exactly as it is. Replace the two-part (plan, stream) address with the catalogue's own product key, so every product in the catalogue is addressable by construction and the two can never drift apart.

NEW (canonical)   /enrollment_form?product=<product_key>      e.g. ?product=kas_3a
LEGACY (keep)     /enrollment_form?plan=<slug>&stream=<stream>   → resolved to a product key server-side

· one key space = the catalogue's own `key` — nothing to keep in sync
· the amount still comes ONLY from the backend resolve (unchanged trust boundary)
· a product that is not `sellable` refuses at resolve, so a stale link cannot charge
· preorder products return a preorder intent (price + fulfilment date + refund terms)
Guard to add with it: a scheduled check that fails when any product marked sellable + live does not resolve to available:true. That is exactly the neet_jee defect, and it would have caught it the day it appeared.
