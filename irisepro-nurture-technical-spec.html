2.2  Journey stage LOCKED — 5 states, strictly forward-only

Orthogonal to temperature. Temperature says how urgently to speak; stage says what to speak about. A HOT lead at new is pushed to a demo; a COLD lead at demo_attended is reminded of what they saw. The two are independent and both are required to select a message.

StageEntered whenThe single goalExit
newContact createdGet a demo bookeddemo booked
demo_bookedSession seat reservedGet them to attendattended, or no-show
demo_attendedAttendance confirmedOpen the joining conversationconversation started
in_conversationOffer discussion beginsClose at the highest fitting tierpaid, or lapsed
enrolledPayment succeeds—Leaves nurture entirely

Two rules the stage model must enforce in code

  1. No offer message may be sent before demo_attended — unless the contact is already an existing customer. The demo does the selling; everything before it exists only to get them into the room. Enforce as a guard on message selection, not as a guideline.
  2. demo_booked → no-show is NOT a downgrade to cold. It needs its own rescue branch (offer the next session immediately). Treating a no-show as a cold lead loses someone who was nearly there. Model it as a distinct event, not merely as absence of the attendance event.

2.3  Offer ladder LOCKED — 5 steps, descend only on a stated objection

Stored as ladder_step on the contact so we can measure where deals actually land, and which objection moved them.

Why step 3 precedes step 4 — encode this, don't leave it to judgement

A money objection is answered with payment terms, not a smaller product. Dropping ₹4,999 → ₹999 loses ₹4,000 and delivers a weaker first experience, which depresses renewal. The part_payment funding source already exists in the system, so step 3 costs us nothing to offer. Only descend to step 4 or 5 when the stated objection is genuinely time, commitment or proof — never money. Worth reporting on: if most deals land at step 5, either the demo is under-selling or the ladder is being offered too early.

Touchpoints that must NEVER be scored LOCKED

For the guardian persona, the following are service data about a child and may not influence any marketing decision, score, tag or audience:

  • The child's task submissions, completions or timings
  • The child's scores, ability profile or scorecard band
  • Anything the child did inside the app

Sending a guardian "your child's scorecard is ready" is fine — that is service. Using how well the child scored to decide who gets an upsell is not. See §8 Rule 1.

4 · The scoring model

One number, 0–100, recomputed after every event.

HOW THE SCORE MOVES

  score starts at 0 when the contact is created
  every touchpoint applies its Δ  (§3)
  score is clamped to 0–100 — it can never go negative or above 100
  score decays with silence, so it reflects interest NOW, not interest ever

  Temperature is derived from score. It is never set by hand.
  Exception: LOST and CUSTOMER are set by events, and override the score entirely.
ScoreTemperatureWhat it means we should do
60–100HOTReach out now. A human may take over
30–59WARMKeep nurturing on cadence
10–29COLDSlow drip. Prove value before asking
0–9NEW or DORMANTNEW if recent; DORMANT if it decayed here

GEETA JI — the band boundaries (60 / 30 / 10) are a starting proposal. Move them once we have 100 real leads and can see where behaviour actually clusters.

GEETA JI — the two decisions hiding in this table

  1. Max touches. After N unanswered messages we stop and let them cool. What is N for each band? Too high and we get blocked — which is permanent and costs us the OTP channel too (§8 Rule 3).
  2. What re-heats someone. A DORMANT lead who suddenly opens a message — does that alone move them back to WARM, or must they reply? This decides whether re-engagement campaigns work or just churn.

6 · The sequences — per stream × persona

A KAS aspirant and a class-5 parent are different conversations, not different tones. Each needs its own sequence.

Start with TWO sequences, not fourteen

7 streams × 2 personas = 14 possible sequences. Do not write 14. Write the two that are genuinely different — kas · self_adult and board_booster · guardian — and let the others start as copies with the stream name swapped. Split a copy into its own sequence only when you can say why it needs different words. Every sequence you create is one you maintain in two languages forever.

6b · The two 5-day sequences — designed as an experiment

We do not know what converts. So the sequences are built to find out, not merely to send. Two sequences, five angles each, order rotated across cohorts.

The design flaw this avoids

A fixed sequence sent to everyone in the same order cannot tell you which message worked. If five messages go out and some people enrol, you have learned that the sequence converts — content and position are confounded.

And at our volume, conversion is the wrong metric. 500 leads ÷ 5 cohorts = 100 each; at a 2–5% enrolment rate that is 2–5 conversions per variant — statistically meaningless. Measure the immediate response (reply / click / booking), which gives 100 samples per variant and produces signal in weeks instead of years.

Our most under-used asset is the 1-year mentoring. ₹4,999 for 21 tasks is a product; ₹4,999 for 21 tasks plus a year of a mentor is a relationship — and it is the only reason the ₹13.70/day arithmetic works at all. If mentoring is presented as a bonus rather than led with, the strongest value argument is being left on the table.

B3 and the zero-fear rule — the distinction that must survive into the copy

Goutam asked for loss aversion: "time lost is a future lost." Recorded brand positioning is "adult is the hero, zero fear", and for the guardian persona, telling a parent their child's future is slipping is parental-anxiety marketing about a child's outcomes.

Resolution: cost of delay is arithmetic, not anxiety. "Abilities compound — a year of mentoring started in August is a different year from one started in March" is simply true and creates urgency without implying failure. One is a calculation; the other is a threat. B3 uses the calculation. Copy review should reject any B3 variant that implies the child falls behind.

Open — needed before B5 can be written

Is there a refund or guarantee? B5 (risk reversal) needs something concrete behind it. Without one it degrades to "here's what happens if you're unhappy" — still worth sending, but far weaker. Risk reversal is usually the highest-leverage change available to a pre-revenue business, and it costs nothing until someone claims it. Product decision, blocks one of ten messages.

Where the canonical text lives LOCKED

In our own system, not in AiSensy's dashboard. AiSensy is where templates are published, not where they are authored. This means: we keep version history, we can see who changed what, and if we ever change BSP the templates are a re-submission rather than a rewrite. Geeta ji's inventory sheet is the source of truth; the dashboard is a copy.

⚠️ Publishing a template is MANUAL — verified 14 Aug 2026

AiSensy's public API reference documents exactly one endpoint: POST backend.aisensy.com/campaign/t1/api/v2, which sends a message through a pre-existing campaign. There is no documented API to create, edit or list templates, and none to create campaigns. Both are done by logging into the dashboard (Manage → Template Message → + New).

What this means operationally. Every message in §7 becomes, once:

  1. Geeta ji writes the copy in the inventory      (our source of truth)
  2. Someone creates the template in the dashboard  MANUAL  × EN and KN
  3. Meta approves                                  24-48 h (auth: 1-2 h)
  4. Someone creates the campaign that wraps it     MANUAL  × EN and KN
  5. Laravel can now send it by campaign name       automated from here

  20 messages → 40 templates + 40 campaigns, hand-created once. Then fully automated.

Tolerable, because it is one-time and low-frequency — Meta's approval delay means automation would save little. But it is a thin API surface for a team building its own orchestration, and it is a fair reason to re-price Gupshup, which is the developer-first option with a fuller API. Our §2 elimination of Gupshup was on platform-fee grounds; given we are building the orchestration ourselves, API depth may be the more important axis. Add to the day-one vendor questions.

8 · Eight rules that cannot be broken

These are enforced in code where possible, so that following them is not a matter of remembering.

#RuleWhy
1The child is never contacted and never profiled. All messages go to the guardian. No child task data, score or behaviour may influence any score, tag, audience or offer India's data protection law prohibits tracking, profiling or targeted advertising directed at children under 18, and consent does not make it permissible. Engineering enforces this as a whitelist on what leaves our system
2No message without a recorded opt-in. Consent is logged with a timestamp and the exact wording shown Required by WhatsApp policy, and it is our evidence if a complaint is ever made
3LOST is permanent and absorbing. No message of any type, ever, until that person opts in again themselves A block is per-number and permanent. If someone blocks us over a marketing message, we also lose the ability to send them their OTP and their scorecard. This is a product failure, not a marketing one
4Classify templates honestly. If it carries an offer, it is marketing Mis-classifying to save cost risks template rejection and account-level damage. The 7× saving is not worth the account
5English + Kannada only at launch. A new language needs a written reason Every language permanently multiplies template authoring, approval, versioning and QA
5bNo offer message before demo_attended, unless the contact is an existing customer The demo does the selling. Enforce as a guard on message selection — a rule that lives only in a process document will be broken by the first campaign that ignores stage
5cDescend the offer ladder only on a STATED objection, and never skip step 3 A money objection is answered with instalments, not a smaller product. Volunteering the ladder unprompted loses the sale we could have had
6One source of truth: our system. Score, temperature and journey state are computed by us. AiSensy holds a copy for filtering audiences and nothing else Two systems that both decide "how hot is this lead" will disagree, and the disagreement will be invisible until it costs a sale

Two things this example exposes for Geeta ji

  1. The score clamps at 100 and then decay barely moves her. She stays HOT for ten days on the strength of one abandoned payment. Is that right, or should an abandoned payment expire faster than ordinary decay?
  2. She used all 3 HOT touches by day 5 and then we went quiet for four days. Is silence correct there, or should she drop to WARM cadence and keep a slower conversation alive?

Neither has an obviously right answer, and both are process decisions rather than technical ones. They are exactly the kind of thing this document exists to surface before it is built.
