The conversion funnel — how a magnet becomes a customer

Every organic campaign should lead with a magnet, not the buy button. The magnet is the hook, the number is the gate, and — because our real edge is the human touch — the 2–5 minute call is the close. Don't build a long email drip; optimise for capture fast → call fast.

1 · HOOK
Organic post / reel / QR / WhatsApp leads with the magnet offer — not "enrol now"
2 · LAND
Tap → /go/<slug> campaign link (carries UTM) → focused landing
3 · CAPTURE
One field — phone + consent. The magnet is the reward for the number
4 · DELIVER
Instant magnet (demo task / sample profile / guide) = product taste
5 · CALL ★
Superfone hot call in 2–5 min — the close. WhatsApp/email only as fallback
6 · ENROL
Convert → UTM ties revenue back to the campaign + magnet
Match magnet ↔ audience

Aspirant ads → the demo task. School-parent ads → the parent quiz. SEO/top-funnel → the stream guides. Same capture form + Lead spine underneath.

Speed is the moat

Capture-to-call in minutes (the SLA watchdog). A hot lead who just did a demo task, called while it's fresh, converts far better than a week-old email.

📈 Why lead with the magnet on organic: a reel that ends "enrol now" converts the ~2% ready to buy and loses the other 98%. A reel that ends "try the free 60-sec demo / get your child's strength report" converts the curious majority into leads we can then call — turning reach into a callable pipeline, and making organic ROI measurable per campaign.
