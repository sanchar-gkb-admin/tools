The problem in one line

In our current architecture each of the four task groups is its own challenge, and a challenge generates its own scorecard. That is four task scorecards. The product specifies one — Scorecard 4. So either Scorecard 4 is a composition of four, or the four never become learner-visible artifacts. Everything below follows from which.

A · What Scorecard 4 actually is

These decide the data model. Nothing downstream can be built until A1 is answered.

A1BLOCKS EVERYTHING
Is Scorecard 4 a composition of the four challenge scorecards, or does the learner never see per-challenge scorecards at all?
Three shapes are possible and they are not interchangeable: (a) four scorecards exist and Scorecard 4 aggregates them; (b) the four are internal only and Scorecard 4 is generated directly from 28 task evaluations; (c) the learner sees all four and a fifth summary. Changes: the data model, what the app stores, and how many artifacts a mentoring session walks through.
A2
If a composition — is it arithmetic (aggregate the ability scores), or narrative (a YASHODARSHI writes it)?
The master says Scorecard 4 is YASHODARSHI-generated but Scorecard 5 is app-assembled. If 4 is also assembled by the app from four rubric outputs, then the human's unit of work is the task, not the scorecard — which changes what the evaluator console asks them to produce.
A3
Can a partial Scorecard 4 exist — say, after Paper 1's Pre and Post only?
A learner will finish Paper 1's four-week-apart tasks long before Paper 2's. Changes: whether the app shows progress or withholds until all 28 are in. The MCQ side already has a designed answer for this shape (S12, "one paper to go").

B · The basis of linking — the part that must be decided first

Goutam: "we first need to understand the basis of this linking." These four are that basis.

B1BLOCKS SCORECARD 5
Do Pre and Post measure the same abilities — so the link is a before/after shift — or different abilities, so they are separate readings?
This is the single most consequential question. If the same, the instrument's headline is the delta and the app must pair every Pre ability to its Post counterpart. If different, there is no delta at all and Scorecard 4 is a union, not a comparison. Everything about how 4 and 5 are drawn depends on it.
B2
Is the shift measured per paper (Pre1→Post1, Pre2→Post2) or across the engagement (Pre1→Post2)?
Per paper gives two independent deltas; across gives one longer arc with the middle two as waypoints. Changes: whether a learner who does only Paper 1 gets a complete story or half of one.
B3
Do the four challenges map to the same ability set, or does each have its own?
If each has its own, an ability may appear in only one of the four and the aggregate is uneven — some abilities backed by 7 tasks, others by 28. The MCQ storyboard already refuses to report an ability below sufficient evidence; the task side needs the same rule.
B4
When one ability appears in more than one challenge, how is it combined — latest, mean, weighted, or kept separate?
Changes: whether a strong Post can mask a weak Pre. If it is a mean, improvement is invisible; if latest, the Pre evidence is discarded.

C · Scorecard 5 — joining marks to abilities

C1BLOCKS SCORECARD 5
How do MCQ marks (Scorecards 1–3) and ability scores (Scorecard 4) combine? They are different units.
One is marks out of 200 with negative marking; the other is a 5-point rubric across abilities. Scorecard 5 "links 1–4" — but a link can be a single composite number, a correlation ("your low Attention to Detail shows up as −12 marks lost to negative marking"), or two panels side by side with no arithmetic between them. Changes: whether we need a conversion, and whether a wrong one can be shipped invisibly.
C2
Does Scorecard 5 carry a single headline number, or is it explicitly a narrative?
It is delivered in a mentoring session, which suggests narrative. But if there is a number, it needs a definition that survives a parent asking "out of what?".
C3
What does Scorecard 5 show that 1–4 do not?
If it is only a cover page over four existing artifacts, it is a view rather than a scorecard — and should not be generated, stored or versioned as one.

D · Evaluation logistics

D1
Does one YASHODARSHI evaluate all 28 tasks, or one per challenge?
"A dedicated YASHODARSHI" is locked for the engagement, but four challenges means four units, and the allocator binds one evaluator per unit. If it must be one person, allocation has to work at engagement level — otherwise four people each see a quarter of the instrument and nobody sees the shift.
D2
What is the SLA for exam-hall evaluation? The storyboard says TBD.
The scan pools are 12h/task and 72h/set. Changes: whether Paper 1's Post tasks must be evaluated before Paper 2 is sat — and therefore whether evaluation is on the learner's critical path.
D3
May a learner sit Paper 2 before Paper 1's tasks are evaluated?
If not, human turnaround gates the product and the SLA becomes a delivery promise rather than an internal target.

E · Sequencing and edge cases

E1
Is the order enforced — Pre → Paper → Post — or advisory?
A Post-Paper Analysis attempted before the paper measures nothing. Changes: whether the app must lock challenges until the preceding step completes.
E2
What happens to a challenge left partially complete — 5 of 7 tasks in the 15-minute window?
The window is one countdown for the set. Changes: whether the abilities behind the missing tasks are reported as low, or as no evidence. Reporting them as low is a measurement error the learner cannot see.
E3
If a learner skips Pre entirely but does Post, is the Post still evaluated and reported?
A Post with no Pre has no baseline. It can still be scored as a standalone reading, but not as a shift.
E4
Minimum evidence — what is the fewest tasks that may back a reported ability?
The MCQ side already suppresses a verdict below significance and shows an evidence count per ability. The task side needs the same floor, or the two halves of one scorecard will apply different standards.

Answer order

If time is short, these four unlock the most: A1 (what Scorecard 4 is) → B1 (same abilities or not) → C1 (how marks join abilities) → D2 (the SLA). Everything else can be decided while building.
