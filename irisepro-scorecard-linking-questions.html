Exam Hall scorecards

What the architecture does today, the gaps that follow, and the questions only Sutirtha can close. Every fact below was measured against production on 26-Aug — none of it is inferred from the spec.

1 · Measured — what exists today

A scorecard is keyed to a CHALLENGE. 
scorecards: id · student_id · challenge_id · unit_id · program_key · tier · json · …
            challenge_id  NOT NULL          unit_id  NULLABLE
• 87 scorecards across 54 units in production.
• Nothing composes a scorecard from other scorecards. No such service exists — every scorecard is generated from evaluated task submissions for one challenge.
• MCQ has no scorecard path at all. mcq_attempts does not exist; mcq_papers/questions/sets/policies are migrated and empty.
• 2A is authored as four challenges — pre_test.1 · post_test.1 · pre_test.2 · post_test.2, 7 tasks each, one 15-minute window per set.

2 · The gaps that follow

Five scorecards are specified. Under today's schema, three of them have nowhere to live and one produces four artifacts instead of one.

The single missing primitive is COMPOSITION. Scorecards 3 and 5 are both defined as derived from other scorecards, and nothing in the system can do that. It is not an Engine-2A gap — Scorecard 3 is needed by 2C, the very first thing that ships on 05 Sep.
And a second: a scorecard cannot exist without a challenge. So either papers become challenges, or challenge_id stops being mandatory, or paper results are not "scorecards" in this table at all. That is an architecture decision, and it is upstream of everything below.

3 · Questions — grounded in those gaps

Q1BLOCKS 05 SEP
Is Scorecard 3 (cumulative) a stored artifact, or a view computed at read time from 1 and 2?
This decides whether we build a composition primitive at all. Stored means a new scorecard row that belongs to no challenge — schema change. A view means it is assembled when the learner opens it and never persisted, which sidesteps the key problem entirely. Needed for 2C on 05 Sep, before any 2A work.
Q2BLOCKS 05 SEP
What is a paper's result keyed to — do the two papers of a set become challenges, or does a paper result live outside the scorecards table?
Today challenge_id is NOT NULL. If papers become challenges, a 2C purchase mints two challenges and the existing scorecard path works unchanged. If not, MCQ results need their own home and the learner then has two kinds of "scorecard" with different plumbing.
Q3BLOCKS 2A
Scorecard 4 is specified as one across 28 tasks, but four challenges produce four. Which is real: (a) four stored, one composed view · (b) four internal, only the composition shown · (c) all four shown to the learner and no Scorecard 4 as such?
Goutam, 26-Aug: "we have 4 challenges so it will produce 4 scorecards." That is what the code does. The spec says one. Changes: what is stored, what is versioned, and how many artifacts a mentoring session walks through.
Q4BLOCKS SC 4 AND 5
Do Pre and Post measure the same abilities — making the link a before/after shift — or different abilities, making it a union?
The most consequential question here. If the same, the headline of the instrument is the delta, and every Pre ability must pair to its Post counterpart. If different, there is no delta and Scorecard 4 is an aggregate. Nothing about how 4 or 5 are drawn can be decided before this.
Q5
Is the shift per paper (Pre1→Post1, Pre2→Post2) or across the engagement (Pre1→Post2)?
Per paper gives two independent deltas; across gives one arc with two waypoints. Changes: whether a learner who completes only Paper 1 has a whole result or half of one.
Q6
When an ability appears in more than one of the four challenges, how is it combined — latest, mean, weighted, or kept separate?
A mean makes improvement invisible; latest discards the Pre evidence. Today each challenge scores independently and nothing reconciles them.
Q7BLOCKS SC 5
How do marks out of 200 (Scorecards 1–3) join 5-point ability scores (Scorecard 4)?
Different units. A "link" could be a composite number, a stated relationship ("low Attention to Detail cost you −12 marks to negative marking"), or two panels with no arithmetic between them. Changes: whether a conversion exists that can be silently wrong.
Q8
Is Scorecard 5 generated and stored, or assembled for the mentoring session and not persisted?
The master says app-assembled, delivered in a session. If it is never stored it is a view and needs no key — which removes the second gap for it entirely.
Q9
Does one YASHODARSHI evaluate all 28 tasks?
"Dedicated" is locked for the engagement, but four challenges means four units, and EvaluatorAllocator binds one evaluator per unit. If one person must see all four, allocation has to move to engagement level — otherwise four people each see a quarter and nobody sees the shift.
Q10
What is the exam-hall evaluation SLA? The storyboard marks it TBD.
Scan pools run 12h/task and 72h/set. Changes: whether Paper 1's Post tasks must be evaluated before Paper 2 is sat — i.e. whether human turnaround sits on the learner's critical path.
Q11
Is the order enforced — Pre → Paper → Post?
A Post-Paper Analysis taken before the paper measures nothing. Changes: whether challenges lock until the preceding step completes.
Q12
A 15-minute window closes with 5 of 7 tasks done. Are the missing abilities low, or no evidence?
Reporting them as low is a measurement error the learner cannot see. The MCQ side already suppresses a verdict below significance and shows an evidence count per ability; the task side needs the same floor or one scorecard applies two standards.

4 · Answer order

Q1 and Q2 are needed for 05 Sep — they are 2C questions, not 2A ones, and they decide whether a composition primitive gets built at all. Q3, Q4 and Q7 unlock 2A. Everything else can be settled during the build.

If Q1 and Q2 are not answered this week, 05 Sep ships a set with two paper results and no cumulative — which is the one artifact a learner buying a "set" is actually paying for.
