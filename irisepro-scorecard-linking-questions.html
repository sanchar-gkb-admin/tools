Exam Hall scorecards

The MCQ scorecard is already fully specified in KAS ASALI SUCCESS SCORECARD™ (tab 24 of the KAS Real Test Series capture). This page separates what that document settles from what it does not, and asks Sutirtha only the second kind.

1 · Already specified — tab 24, do not re-ask

One scorecard per exam, where an exam = Paper 1 + Paper 2 (a set). Twelve sections, not three separate artifacts.

• §1 at-a-glance — Paper 1 / Paper 2 / Overall: max marks, net, %, attempted, correct, incorrect, left, negative marks, accuracy. Combined Accuracy = Total Correct ÷ Total Attempted × 100.
• §2–§7 — per paper: performance, 11 Critical Success Abilities, 4 Behavioural Success Abilities.
• §8–§9 the combining rule, explicitly: Overall Ability Score = (Paper 1 + Paper 2) ÷ 2, for both the 11 critical and the 4 behavioural abilities.
• Three benchmarks retained — Developmental · ASALI Champion · KAS ASALI Champion — each with a signed gap per ability.
• §10 — benchmark achievement summary X/11, X/4, X/15, plus closest and largest gap.
• §11 — exam-to-exam progress, from Exam 2 onward only; comparison unit is Exam = Paper 1 + Paper 2.
• §12 — Success Doctor's Summary & Recommended Success Path™.

3 · Questions

Q1BLOCKS 2A
Do the 28 tasks measure the same 15 abilities as the papers — the 11 Critical + 4 Behavioural — or a different set?
If the same, the tasks are a second reading of one instrument and everything in §8–§10 extends naturally. If different, 2A's scorecard carries two ability vocabularies and §10's "X/15" no longer describes the whole picture. Everything else here depends on this answer.
Q2BLOCKS 2A
If the same 15 — how does a task ability score combine with the paper ability score?
§8 gives the paper rule: (Paper 1 + Paper 2) ÷ 2. Is the overall then (MCQ + Task) ÷ 2, a weighted blend, or do they stay side by side and never merge? Changes: whether one number can be reported per ability, or two must be.
Q3BLOCKS 2A
Unit conversion: paper abilities are reported as percentages (84, 90, 87.0%). The task rubric is a 5-point scale. What is the mapping?
A 5-point rubric cannot be averaged with a percentage without a stated conversion, and an implicit one (×20) is a modelling decision, not arithmetic. Changes: whether task and paper abilities can share a row in §8 at all.
Q4
Do the 28 tasks carry the same three benchmarks — Developmental, ASALI Champion, KAS ASALI Champion — with signed gaps?
§8–§10 are built entirely on those three. If tasks have no benchmarks, the task rows in the scorecard have empty gap columns and §10's X/15 counts only the MCQ half.
Q5BLOCKS 2A
Is the Pre → Post shift a reported measure, and if so where does it sit?
§11 exists for exam-to-exam progression, from Exam 2 onward. But 2A is a single exam containing four task groups — Pre1, Post1, Pre2, Post2. That is a within-exam progression §11 does not describe. If the Pre→Post delta is the thing 2A sells, it needs its own section, and a rule for whether it is Pre1→Post1 and Pre2→Post2, or Pre1→Post2.
Q6
Is Scorecard 4 a section of the same document, or a separate scorecard?
Tab 24 is one document with twelve sections. If the tasks become §13–§15 of it, there is one scorecard per exam and "5 scorecards" is a description of sections. Four challenges produce four scorecards today — one per challenge. The answer decides whether those four are composed into one view, or replaced by it.
Q7
A 15-minute window closes with 5 of 7 tasks done. Are the missing abilities weak, or no evidence?
§8 reports every ability with a number and a gap. A partially-evidenced ability reported as a low score is a measurement error the candidate cannot see. Tab 24 has no "insufficient evidence" state.
Q8
Does one YASHODARSHI evaluate all 28 tasks, and what is the SLA?
"A dedicated YASHODARSHI" is locked, but four challenges means four units and the allocator binds one evaluator per unit. The exam-hall pool's SLA is marked TBD in the storyboard. Changes: whether human turnaround sits between Paper 1 and Paper 2 on the candidate's critical path.

4 · Answer order

Q1 → Q2 → Q3 in that order. Q1 decides whether there is one instrument or two; Q2 the arithmetic; Q3 whether the arithmetic is even possible in shared units. Q5 next, because the Pre→Post shift may be the headline of 2A and has no home in the current format.
