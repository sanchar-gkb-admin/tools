Part-payment scorecard lock

₹4999 only. A part-paying student enrols, onboards, submits every task and is evaluated. Their scorecard shows the first 3 task scores then locks the rest behind a pay-to-unlock CTA; the leaderboard and certificate stay fully locked until paid. Progress bar stays visible. An admin clicks "Mark scorecard settled" to unlock everything.
