RULE

What inbound changes

  • Any reply resets the unanswered counter to zero
  • Lead exits the scheduled sequence and is handled conversationally
  • Assistant may qualify, answer and route — no template needed
  • If the window closes unanswered, the lead returns to its track — never restarts it
  • A STOP / opt-out reply ends everything, permanently

Inbound outranks every scheduled track. A person asking a question must never receive a scheduled broadcast in the same hour.

07 Stop conditions & channel health

What ends a sequence

Hard stop

The instant a payment lands, every sequence ends for that person. A paying customer receiving "enrol now" is worse than no message at all.

Frequency discipline

  • Max 3 unanswered messages, then pause — not daily-forever
  • Any reply resets the counter and hands to the assistant
  • One voice attempt per lead in 48h, not per day
  • Quiet hours — nothing before 9 AM or after 9 PM
  • After 7 days silent: one re-entry message only, never a fresh sequence

Why it matters

  • Meta caps marketing templates per recipient; utility is uncapped
  • Users opt out of marketing while staying opted in to utility
  • Blocks and unanswered sends degrade quality rating → throttling
  • Only 4 of 9 templates are marketing — that is the whole exposed surface
  • The channel this campaign runs on is the channel it can destroy

09 Verify before shipping

Five assumptions this rests on

Each drawn from the page, not from data. Each cheap to check, expensive to get wrong.

Could invalidate the design

  • Does the nightly webinar convert? The whole funnel points at it because the CTAs do. Check attendance + enrolments within 72h.
  • Is the webinar resilient? One person, every night. What happens when he travels?
  • Who sends the webinar link? The site already promises to. Superfone must not double-send.

Claims to substantiate

  • The 400-mark loss table is labelled "estimated". Outbound at scale invites scrutiny a landing page does not.
  • 15,000+ students and the 94–95% benchmarks — same exposure.
  • Kannada must come from KMM's own copy, never machine-translated from English.
