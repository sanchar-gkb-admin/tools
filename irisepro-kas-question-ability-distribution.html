3 · What the map says

Finding 1 — the papers are one ability wearing a costume. GS-1 puts 60 of 100 questions on Comprehension as primary; GS-2 puts 50 of 100. Add the weighted secondary and tertiary load and Comprehension carries 37.3 of GS-1's 100 demand units and 29.2 of GS-2's. The next-largest in GS-1 is Attention to Detail at 16.5. GS-1 uses just six distinct primary abilities across a hundred questions, and three of them appear on 5 questions between them. A 64-ability framework is being reported off a paper that meaningfully exercises about four.
Finding 2 — three of the KAS Top 15 have zero content evidence in either paper. Steadfastness, Discipline and Conflict Resolution appear as the primary, secondary or tertiary ability of not one question in 200. Structure and Time management have 0.3 demand units each — a single secondary or tertiary tag. These cannot be reported as measured from content, at any attempt count, ever. GS-1 §5D does apply Time management, Efficiency, Discipline, Steadfastness and Decision-making as a behavioural overlay to all 100 questions — but that is timing and attempt data, a different channel with a different denominator, and the design's own invariant I9 walls it out of the ability denominator.

Demand units = 0.5 primary + 0.3 secondary + 0.2 tertiary, the documents' own weighting. Half-width is the 95% binomial interval at p = 0.5 — the most generous case.

Finding 3 — the benchmark tiers sit 2–3 points apart. Nothing in this table resolves them. Even pooling both papers, the best-measured ability in the entire framework — Comprehension, k = 66.5 — carries ±12.0 points at one attempt and ±6.9 at three. Critical & Analytical Thinking is ±12.1 at three attempts; Problem-solving ±16.9; Accountability ±27.6. Against benchmark tiers separated by 2–3 points, every one of these intervals spans all three tiers with room to spare. Reporting a student as sitting at the Competitive tier rather than the Developmental one is, on this content, not a measurement.
Finding 4 — the two papers are not interchangeable, so pooling is not optional. GS-1 has 6 distinct primary abilities, GS-2 has 12. Problem-solving is 3.7 units in GS-1 and 7.5 in GS-2; Mental Dexterity 0.2 versus 3.1; Memory 16.1 versus 12.7. A student who sits only GS-1 cannot be given a Problem-solving number on the same footing as one who sat both. This is the concrete argument for the ability_profile_scope flag — and it argues that series should be the default, not paper.
Finding 5 — the answer keys, re-verified from these very rows. GS-1's key is A=25 B=25 C=25 D=25 and 100 of 100 answers match a strict A,B,C,D cycle — zero deviations. GS-2's is A=61 B=19 C=18 D=2, with a run of 27 consecutive identical answers. Marking "A" on every GS-2 question scores 112.25 / 200. Any ability inference drawn from these two papers as they stand is confounded by answer position before it begins.
Finding 6 — area is positionally blocked, which is the blueprint-leak surface. GS-1's 16 areas fall into 22 contiguous runs (Q1–12 Current/International, Q13–17 History-India, Q22–29 Indian National Movement, Q31–42 Karnataka History/Culture …); GS-2 is blocked by section (Q1–40 Karnataka, Q41–70 Science/Environment, Q71–100 Mental Ability). Since the design fixes slot identity to (paper_id, seq_no) and shuffles only within an area, position predicts area and area predicts ability for every student on every attempt, permanently. Combined with Finding 1, a student who learns that Q1–12 is Comprehension can allocate time by ability. Per-student slot→position randomisation is the fix, and it costs nothing to do now.

4 · What this changes in the design

EvidenceConsequence
3 of the Top 15 have zero content demand; 2 more have 0.3 unitsThe card must render the not_assessed and insufficient states for these by construction, not as an edge case. Five of fifteen headline abilities can never carry a number from content.
Comprehension is 30–37% of both papers' demandBlueprint targets belong in the authoring admin as a constraint, not a report. If a slot's ability set is the unit of design, the paper's ability distribution is a design decision that is currently being made by accident.
k ranges from 66.5 down to 0.3 across the Top 15Abilities cannot share a visual weight on the profile screen. Evidence count per ability has to be on the card.
GS-1 and GS-2 have different ability shapesability_profile_scope = series is the defensible default; paper produces two profiles that are not comparable to each other.
Both answer keys are structurally predictableRegenerate both keys before any of this content seeds a bank. The authoring admin's letter-distribution and positional-autocorrelation gates must fail on these two papers — that is their acceptance test.
Area is positionally blockedRandomise slot→position per student. Shuffling within an area does not hide a blueprint that is itself blocked by area.
