The Root Rule
A child performs at the level of their lowest activated centre — not their highest ability.
जड़ बंद है, तो ऊपर कुछ नहीं खुलेगा।
If the root is dormant, nothing above it can open.
Why this makes ASLI64 necessary

If performance were an average, a parent could guess where to intervene and tuition would be a fair bet. Because it is a minimum, you cannot know where to act without measuring all 64. The Root Rule is what turns the ₹4,999 scan from “more detail” into “the only way to find the floor”.

Why “activation”, not “unblocking”

“Blocked” is a deficit frame — it says something is wrong with the child, fighting p4’s promise to reduce blame and pressure. Dormant → activated says nothing is broken, something is latent. Same diagnosis, opposite emotional charge. And a seed is never blocked — it is dormant until fed. That is already your tree.

Open decisions for Goutam

1 · Tagline — DECIDED

was: SCAN • SHOW • STRENGTHEN • SUCCEED
SCAN • RECOGNISE • ACTIVATE • REALISE

Each verb owns one layer: SCAN the 64 · RECOGNISE the 7 · ACTIVATE the lowest · REALISE the child. REALISE is the only English verb meaning both to grasp and to make actual — and it lands on Sahasrara, the seat of self-realisation. Applied to the frames above.

2 · Order of ⑤ and ⑥

Classically Expression sits below Insight, so the Root Rule asserts a child cannot deepen understanding without articulating it — which makes “fix comprehension first” wrong advice. A useful contrast against tuition, or a fight you don’t want.

3 · Sanskrit subtitles

Shown throughout. They lift cleanly for institutional/CBSE decks without redrawing anything — the English names carry the meaning alone.

4 · Per-centre ability counts

Only the Top 15 are named in v29. Exact counts per centre need the full 64 list before any number goes on a page.
