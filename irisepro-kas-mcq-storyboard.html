A · Authoring — desktop

The author's unit of work is a slot: a group of different questions sharing one topic, cognitive demand, ability set and difficulty band. Abilities are set once on the slot; a form never declares its own.

A01 · Blueprint — what's missing

The landing screen. One cell per slot, shaded by how full it is. Answers "where do I work next" before anything is opened.

Screen 10 is the product. Marks tell a student what they scored. The trajectory tells them whether the coaching is working — and an ability that stays flat across four blocks of feedback is a different problem from one that climbs. That distinction is only visible because attempts are grouped into blocks with a reveal between them.
