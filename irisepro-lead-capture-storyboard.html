Lead capture — the missing pieces

The storefront is already live, so the leaks are active: every dismissed checkout and browse-and-bounce is captured nowhere server-side. This storyboard is the design to close that — one reusable capture component, the delivery pipeline that hands a hot lead to Superfone in minutes, and the attribution that ties it to revenue. Mobile-first and desktop throughout. Nothing here is built yet — review & approve, then we build.

Phone is king

Superfone calls it — Indian mobile, normalised to canonical E.164 (PhoneNumber::e164) so a lead here dedups with the same person on any other page. Email is secondary/optional.

Role selector = the DPDPA gate

aspirant / parent — school streams are framed "a parent's number." Consent + role decide who Superfone lawfully calls. No role, no consent → not a callable lead.

Required consent checkbox

A lead only exists once consent is ticked — the lawful basis to call. A pre-consent keystroke is at most an anonymous PostHog event, never a lead.

WhatsApp opt-in, logged like consent

Stored with timestamp + source; gates any WhatsApp send. Defaults to the same number.

3b · Mobile-first & desktop — same component, different feel

📱 Mobile primary

  • Form = bottom sheet. Thumb-reachable, slides up from the bottom; single column; big tap targets; numeric keypad on the phone field.
  • Modal triggers: back-button intercept · fast scroll-up · visibilitychange (tab/app switch). No exit-intent (no cursor on touch).
  • Floating WhatsApp pinned bottom-right, above the fold of the thumb zone.
  • One field per glance; consent + WA opt-in stacked; sticky submit.

🖥 Desktop secondary

  • Form = centered sheet (modal) or inline card in the section; two-up layout where space allows.
  • Modal triggers: exit-intent (cursor to browser chrome) · scroll ≥50% · dwell ≥35s · pricing-viewed.
  • WhatsApp as a corner pill / inline in the success state — less dominant than on mobile.
  • Hover states, keyboard nav + visible focus rings; wider inline embeds.

5 · The delivery pipeline (Phase 2) — capture → hand off → prove it

LeadCaptureForm→ POST /api/lead→ Lead spine (Enquiry / CheckoutAttempt — one record)→ SyndicateLead job · queued · seconds→ LeadSink port→ Superfone · calls in 2–5 min

🔌 LeadSink — swappable driver

One port push(Lead):Ack. Drivers: Null/Log (works today, no dep) → Pabbly (reuse) → Superfone (drop-in when their API + ack lands). Upstream never changes.

⏱ SLA watchdog

Scheduled: any hot lead not acked in ~3 min → OpsAlert::critical to Slack. Turns "2–5 min" from a hope into a guarantee. We never build calling — Superfone owns it.

＋ every capture fires PostHog contact_entered + Meta Pixel Lead. Idempotent · retry+backoff · ::failed → OpsAlert.

6 · Attribution (Phase 3) — tie a lead to campaign → revenue

first-touch UTM (set-once) last-touch UTM (each session) persisted on → Lead + payment row + PostHog person → campaign → ₹ revenue ROI
Both models stored so the dashboard attributes revenue by first-touch (awareness) and last-touch (closing) with no re-instrumenting. The campaign link (/go/{slug}, already built) embeds ?exam=&plan= so a lead auto-tags stream/plan — Superfone opens with the right pitch.
