We're hiring

UI/UX Designer

YASHHO · Bengaluru · Full-time

Turn what YASHHO means into designs the team can build from.

Why this role exists

Right now our founder describes what he wants, and an engineer tries to build it. Sometimes that works. Often it doesn't, because the thing in his head was never written down anywhere an engineer could read it.

We want that gap closed by a designer. You sit between the people who hold the vision and the people who ship the code. Everything engineering builds should come from your Figma rather than from a voice note. You'll ask plenty of questions along the way, and so will they. What we're trying to avoid is engineering having to guess.

What you'd own

The soul of the thing

YASHHO measures and strengthens the 64 abilities behind a child's success. That's a genuinely different idea from tuition or coaching, and our site should feel different too. Right now it mostly doesn't. Your job is to make someone feel what we're about within seconds of landing, before they've read a word.

Turning abstract into concrete

You'll get ideas as conversations, sketches on paper, and long voice notes. You turn those into storyboards and screen flows that show the whole journey. If a reviewer can't see what the user sees, it isn't done.

The design language

One system, one library, covering both yashho.com and the student app. Today they look like two products from two companies. You'd fix that and then keep it fixed.

The words too

Not just where the text sits, but what it says. You'll work with our content lead on this, but the quality of what a visitor reads is yours as much as what they look at.

Being the translator

Founders speak in intent. Engineers need specifics. You convert one into the other, and you're allowed to push back in both directions.

What matters most to us

Tell us what drives you

We'd rather hire someone with a point of view than someone who's neutral and available. What kind of work makes you want to start early?

Ownership

Nobody will chase you. If something looks wrong on the site at midnight, we want the person who noticed to be you.

Comfort with change

We change our minds. Sometimes because we learn something, sometimes because a school tells us we got it wrong. If that frustrates you, this will be a hard place to work.

Storyboarding

This is the skill we'll test hardest. Take something vague and show it as a sequence of real screens, on mobile and desktop, with the states that matter.

You use AI properly

Not to generate slop faster. To get from idea to something you can look at in an afternoon instead of a week, so you spend your time on the thinking that actually needs a person. Tell us how you already work this way.

Who you are

Six to nine years designing products people actually used. You've built a design system that outlived the project it started in. You've been the first or only designer somewhere, so ambiguity doesn't scare you. Strong Figma. You can write.

Kannada isn't required, but understanding the families we serve helps a lot. We're building for parents in Karnataka first, and a lot of them read Kannada before English.

Who you probably aren't

Someone who needs a fully-written brief. Someone who wants to manage a team. Someone whose portfolio is beautiful but who can't explain why any of it is the way it is.
