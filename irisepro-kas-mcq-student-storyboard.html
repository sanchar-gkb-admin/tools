KAS MCQ — the student experience

Seventeen screens. A four-lens red team rewrote the first six and found the rest.

The SET is the unit. One set = GS-1 + GS-2 = 200 questions. ₹1,499 buys one set; ₹4,500/month buys eight, all different, no question repeated across any of them. Each set yields its own ability profile, they aggregate into a standing profile, and the progression runs set 1 → 8. That replaces the old 3 / 7 / 21-attempt ladder and cuts the content obligation from 4,400 forms to 1,800.

1 · The timer

Decided here because it shapes every screen in the attempt. There is no per-question timer.

QuestionAnswer
Per-question countdown?No. "00:47" beside "Q 23 / 100" reads as seconds-on-this-question or minutes-left. In a 120-minute paper the one number that must never be ambiguous is time remaining.
What replaces itA wall clock (exact, always) and one bar carrying two facts: fill = questions answered, tick = where the clock says you should be. The gap between them is the message.
Overspend at 60s / 72sThe aggregate signal already covers it — overspend and your fill falls behind the tick. If an immediate cue is wanted, the leading edge of the fill warms cream → amber across 60→72s and resets on advance. One element, no text, no new furniture.
Beep or haptic?No — on measurement grounds. Audio and vibration don't fire identically across devices and OS settings. If Time management is a measured ability and one student gets a buzz while another doesn't, part of that score measures what phone they own. Colour renders everywhere.
Flagmcq.pace_signal ∈ {none · ambient · ambient_plus_overspend}, stamped on the card. We measure time management under this setting; a card that doesn't say which setting produced it is uninterpretable.
What could not be reused. task-details' timer calls expireTask → markExpired() at zero — status expired, zero evaluation, and a card reading "marked as not attempted". On a two-hour paper that voids the exam at the buzzer. Its second-tab handler (task:softlock) permanently halts the countdown. What is worth reusing is the ~40-line skew-proof anchoring pattern — client anchors Date.now() + remaining, never the absolute deadline, re-syncs on foreground — not the partial.

8 · Still open

Eight decisions the screens are drawn around rather than on. Each changes what ships.

#QuestionOwner
1Which estimand does an ability score name — performance on this set's slots, or ability in general?kmm
2Is risk-taking part of the ability construct? Guessing is currently worth +10 to +14pp — 2.5 to 3.5 benchmark tiers.kmm
3Does "never show an Event Score" forbid a paper mark, or only a per-question one?kmm / founder
4Three benchmark tiers or one — the instrument resolves none of them separately (needs k ≥ 4,268).kmm
4bWeights must vary slot to slot. An ability weighted the same everywhere reports the student's overall score under an ability's name. Needs stating to kmm before the file is finalised, with CV ≥ 0.5 as the acceptance gate.kmm
4ck_min — the least effective evidence that may back a reported ability.kmm
5Rank on MCQ: yes or no. Silence ships the existing leaderboard tab, padded with Sample A filler rows.founder
6Does familiarity-driven speed count as improved time management? It rises for every student regardless of ability.kmm
7pace_signal default — ambient or ambient_plus_overspend.founder
8Bilingual or English-only — kmm is authoring fresh papers, so this costs nothing today and a migration later.founder
