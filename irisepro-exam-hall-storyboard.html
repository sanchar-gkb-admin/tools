Why derive rather than make tier conditional. A required_if rule can be satisfied wrongly — an author can pick exam_hall and still send a tier, and validation is the only thing standing in the way. 07Segregating the evaluation pool

A third pool, not a filter. Allocation, capacity accounting and the queue all read pool, so one value segregates every one of them at once.

Why a pool and not a filter on the queue. A filter fixes what a human sees. EvaluatorAllocator and CapacityMonitor both branch on pool(), so without a third value the work is still allocated into the scan pool and still counted against the ₹1,999 cohort — the screen would look right while the routing stayed wrong.
Interim, deliberately. The destination is resolving SLA from the grant — a promise made to a buyer, not a property of a challenge. That is blocked: payment_grants has 0 rows on prod and all 64 payments predate the working writer. The pool segregates correctly today without waiting for it.
