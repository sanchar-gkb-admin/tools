KAS MCQ — the complete student journey

2C (one set) and 2B (four sets a month), coexisting with the 1A/1B/1C scans. Every state a learner can be in, including the ones that have no screen today.

What this adds. The existing 17-screen storyboard begins at the set list and ends at the ability profile. It assumes a booked seat that nothing in the journey creates, and it has no screen for a learner who missed a sitting, who arrives late, who wants to sit again, whose language we cannot serve, or who holds a scan and an exam series at once. Those are not edge cases — each is a real person on a real day, and a journey without them is a demo.
