YASHHO × Gupshupmessaging use cases

How YASHHO plans to use Gupshup for WhatsApp and SMS from launch, with RCS as a candidate after launch. Pilot and beta use email only. Every name, number and order id below is a fake sample.

The rule: context decides the channel. No reply needed → email (and WhatsApp for receipts and urgent notices). Reply needed → WhatsApp with buttons. Parents: WhatsApp → SMS after 15 min → email, all configurable on our side. Quiet hours 21:00–09:00 except urgent. Opt-out always wins. No child details on WhatsApp, SMS or RCS. Team messages stay in our app.

Where our design may not fit

Please tell us if any of these is not possible on Gupshup.

AreaOur concern
Our-side fallback vs vendor auto-fallbackWe decide WhatsApp -> SMS -> email (RCS later) in our own configurable table. If Gupshup's auto-fallback cannot be turned off per message, parents may get the same message twice.
15-minute 'undelivered' ruleWhatsApp shows 'delivered' only when the phone is online. A phone that comes online after 15 minutes may get both the WhatsApp and the SMS.
Slot confirmation and parent check as utilityMeta may class question-style templates as marketing (higher price, stricter opt-in), or re-classify later.
One confirmation after STOPOur rule sends exactly one opt-out confirmation. A platform that blocks all sends instantly after STOP would stop it; SMS STOP is not a standard Indian feature (opt-outs are mostly TRAI/DND).
Replies to parents outside 24 hoursFree-form replies are only allowed inside WhatsApp's 24-hour window; after that we must use an approved template.
Kannada everywhereEvery template needs English and Kannada versions, each approved separately (WhatsApp) and registered separately (DLT).
SMS wording changesOur message wording is configuration we can change without code; on SMS each change likely needs DLT re-registration first.
Data in India and erasureWe need India residency, short retention and erasure for DPDP; Meta's own retention for WhatsApp may be outside Gupshup's control.
Quiet hoursIf Gupshup has no send-window feature we enforce 21:00-09:00 ourselves (planned anyway).
RCS not yetRCS reach on Airtel/Vi and iPhone in India is unclear; RCS is a candidate for after launch, not a launch dependency.

Open questions for Gupshup

We are not fully sure about the integration until these are answered. 23 are blockers for our launch.

Platform and API

  1. BlockerIs there ONE API and one set of credentials for SMS, WhatsApp and RCS, with one message id and one status model across channels?
  2. BlockerCan your automatic channel fallback (WhatsApp -> RCS -> SMS) be switched OFF per message or per account? We run fallback on our side, in an order we configure; if both sides fall back, the parent gets duplicates.
  3. ImportantIf we keep our own fallback: how soon after sending do you report 'delivered' for WhatsApp? We wait 15 minutes (configurable) before trying SMS. What happens if WhatsApp delivers after we have already sent the SMS?
  4. ImportantRate limits and throughput: messages per second per channel, burst limits, queueing when limits are hit, and what error we get.
  5. ImportantSandbox and test numbers: can we test WhatsApp templates, buttons, SMS and webhooks end to end before go-live, without real parents?
  6. Nice to knowClient libraries for PHP/Laravel, and an OpenAPI spec?

WhatsApp onboarding

  1. BlockerExact steps and timeline for WhatsApp Business onboarding through Gupshup as BSP: Meta Business verification, display name approval, the green tick (official business account) - what do you do and what must we do?
  2. BlockerCan we use an existing YASHHO phone number for the WhatsApp API? What happens to the WhatsApp Business app history on that number, and is there downtime during migration?
  3. ImportantCan we have more than one number (our support call line and our WhatsApp line will differ), and separate numbers per use (for example payments vs support) under one account?

WhatsApp templates

  1. BlockerTemplate approval: how long does Meta approval take through you, who drafts and submits, and what happens on rejection? We need English and Kannada for every template.
  2. BlockerCategory rules: will our slot confirmation (U5) and parent check (U6) be accepted as UTILITY, or classed as marketing? Can Meta re-classify an approved template later, and do you notify us?
  3. BlockerPricing in India today: per-message or per-conversation? Price per utility, marketing, authentication and service message, and whether replies inside the 24-hour window are free.
  4. BlockerInteractive messages: quick-reply buttons, URL buttons with a dynamic per-order link (Pay now, Rate us), list messages, document header (PDF receipt). Which are supported in templates (business-started) vs session messages?
  5. ImportantWhatsApp Flows or in-chat payments: supported through you in India? (Today we plan a URL button to our own payment page.)
  6. BlockerUtility-template rules for no-reply notices (receipt, refund, slot changed): what wording keeps them in the utility category, and can a utility template carry a URL button and a PDF?
  7. ImportantAny limits or rules on sending the SAME notice on WhatsApp and on SMS or email at the same moment (we do this for urgent notices and receipts)? Any de-duplication on your side we must turn off?
  8. BlockerPricing of utility vs marketing for these notices, and whether a utility message sent inside an open 24-hour window is free.

Webhooks and replies

  1. BlockerInbound webhooks: payload format for text replies, button replies (which button, linked to which message we sent), media, and STOP. Please share sample payloads.
  2. BlockerWebhook security: are webhooks signed (HMAC or similar) so we can verify they came from you? Fixed source IPs?
  3. BlockerDelivery and read receipts for every channel: which statuses (sent, delivered, read, failed), failure reason codes, and ordering guarantees.
  4. ImportantWebhook retries: how many, over how long, back-off, and can we replay missed events? Is each event idempotent (unique event id)?

Opt-out and consent

  1. BlockerSTOP handling: do you catch STOP / unsubscribe keywords on WhatsApp and SMS? Do you block further sends yourself, and how is your opt-out list synced to ours (webhook and API)? Our rule: opt-out always wins.
  2. BlockerAfter a parent opts out we send exactly ONE confirmation, then nothing. Will your platform allow that one message, or block it?
  3. ImportantQuiet hours: do you support send windows (we keep 21:00-09:00 quiet except urgent payment, refund and complaint messages), or must we enforce them? We plan to enforce on our side either way.

DLT (SMS)

What we already understand (to confirm with Gupshup): DLT registration is YASHHO's own - YASHHO is the Principal Entity. The vendor helps file it and is bound to our entity as the telemarketer. Each SMS needs a registered header and an exact registered template.
  1. BlockerPrincipal Entity (PE) registration: what documents does YASHHO need (GST, PAN, CIN, authorisation letter, others), on which operator portal (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL), what fees, and how long?
  2. BlockerTelemarketer binding: do you bind yourselves as our telemarketer (TM) on our PE account? Do you FILE the PE, headers and templates for us, or only guide us? What must a YASHHO person do personally, and who must sign?
  3. BlockerHeaders (sender IDs): 6-character header(s) for transactional / service vs promotional. Can we have more than one (for example one for payments, one for reminders)? Timeline per header.
  4. BlockerContent templates: who drafts and registers each SMS template (our U3, U4 fallbacks and others), variable rules, approval time, and are edits a full re-registration (every wording change)?
  5. BlockerTotal time to be live end to end: PE + headers + templates + Gupshup onboarding + WhatsApp approval. We have 2-3 weeks before launch - is that realistic?
  6. ImportantConsent templates / consent registration for promotional or service-explicit messages, and how TRAI preferences / opt-outs sync to us.
  7. ImportantScrubbing and rejection: what happens when a message does not match a registered template? Do we get a failure webhook with a reason code?
  8. ImportantDoes DLT also apply to RCS templates? WhatsApp needs no DLT (please confirm), but the SMS fallback copy of each WhatsApp message must be DLT-registered - correct?
  9. Nice to knowSMS sender ID: can we keep one header like YASHHO across all operators? Unicode (Kannada) SMS: supported, and how are segments counted?
