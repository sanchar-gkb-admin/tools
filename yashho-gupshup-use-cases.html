YASHHO × GupshupWhatsApp and SMSuse cases

How YASHHO will use Gupshup for WhatsApp and SMS: sign-in codes, transactional messages and reminders, and the replies that come back into our inbox.

How YASHHO uses WhatsApp (and SMS through Gupshup): for parents and students only, and only for (1) messages after registration, (2) payment-related messages, and (3) replies to a message the parent or student started. Outbound messages are important transactional messages (sign-in codes, payment link, receipt, failed payment and its follow-ups, the parent check after a paid sale, the opt-out confirmation) and reminders (scan progress, webinars). Team messaging does not use Gupshup. Partner messages from personal phones are out of scope.

The scan: the ASLI64™ Master Success Scan (Rs 4,999 + 18% GST) is an at-home, online, expert-guided, parent-involved programme: 21 structured tasks (about 12 minutes each, about 5 hours in total) done in the YASHHO app. It measures 64 Essential Success Abilities (AACE64: Attitude, Aptitude, Communication, Execution) and produces a 49-page Personalized Success Path Chart. SCAN → SHOW → STRENGTHEN → SUCCEED. It has no fixed time or place: it must be started within 7 days of payment and is complete at 18 or more of 21 tasks.

All names, numbers and order ids are examples. Every screen cites its source: a use case in our CRM register, a Goutam ruling of 18 Nov 2026, or the code that sends the message today. No child names on WhatsApp or SMS. Opt-out always wins. Quiet hours 21:00–09:00 except urgent.

Where our design may not fit

Please tell us if any of these is not possible on Gupshup.

AreaOur concern
Our-side fallback vs vendor auto-fallbackWe decide WhatsApp -> SMS in our own configurable table. If Gupshup's auto-fallback cannot be turned off per message, parents may get the same message twice.
15-minute 'undelivered' ruleWhatsApp shows 'delivered' only when the phone is online. A phone that comes online after 15 minutes may get both the WhatsApp and the SMS.
Parent check as utilityMeta may put a question-style template (U5) in a different category than utility, or re-categorise it later.
STOP on SMSAfter STOP we send exactly one confirmation, then nothing on any channel (Goutam ruling 18-Nov-2026, R69 F9). A platform that blocks all sends instantly after STOP would stop it. SMS STOP is not a standard Indian feature (opt-outs are mostly TRAI/DND), so we need your opt-out events on every channel.
Replies to parents outside 24 hoursFree-form replies are only allowed inside WhatsApp's 24-hour window; after that we must use an approved template.
Kannada everywhereEvery template needs English and Kannada versions, each approved separately (WhatsApp) and registered separately (DLT).
SMS wording changesOur message wording is configuration we can change without code; on SMS each change likely needs DLT re-registration first.
Data in India and erasureWe need India residency, short retention and erasure for DPDP; Meta's own retention for WhatsApp may be outside Gupshup's control.
Quiet hoursIf Gupshup has no send-window feature we enforce 21:00-09:00 ourselves (planned anyway).

Open questions for Gupshup

We are not fully sure about the integration until these are answered. 30 are blockers for going live.

Platform and API

  1. BlockerIs there ONE API and one set of credentials for SMS and WhatsApp, with one message id and one status model across channels?
  2. BlockerCan your automatic channel fallback (WhatsApp -> SMS) be switched OFF per message or per account? We run fallback on our side, in an order we configure; if both sides fall back, the parent gets duplicates.
  3. ImportantIf we keep our own fallback: how soon after sending do you report 'delivered' for WhatsApp? We wait 15 minutes (configurable) before trying SMS. What happens if WhatsApp delivers after we have already sent the SMS?
  4. ImportantRate limits and throughput: messages per second per channel, burst limits, queueing when limits are hit, and what error we get.
  5. ImportantSandbox and test numbers: can we test WhatsApp templates, buttons, SMS and webhooks end to end before go-live, without real parents?
  6. Nice to knowClient libraries for PHP/Laravel, and an OpenAPI spec?
  7. BlockerCorrelation: is your message id returned synchronously on send, and can we pass our own reference / metadata and get it echoed on every status and reply webhook?
  8. BlockerAPI-only replies: can many of our staff reply from one WhatsApp number purely through the API (our own inbox), with no seats in your inbox product required?
  9. ImportantSeparate test and live accounts and numbers (staging vs production)?

WhatsApp onboarding

  1. BlockerExact steps and timeline for WhatsApp Business onboarding through Gupshup as BSP: Meta Business verification, display name approval, the green tick (official business account) - what do you do and what must we do?
  2. BlockerCan we use an existing YASHHO phone number for the WhatsApp API? What happens to the WhatsApp Business app history on that number, and is there downtime during migration?
  3. ImportantCan we have more than one number (our support call line and our WhatsApp line will differ), and separate numbers per use (for example payments vs support) under one account?
  4. BlockerMeta messaging limits per number (tiers of 250 / 1k / 10k a day, quality rating): what do you do to help us raise them before go-live, and how does your platform handle an urgent burst over the limit?

WhatsApp templates

  6. BlockerUtility templates for no-reply notices (receipt, failed payment, scan progress, Success Path Chart ready): what wording guidance do you give so Meta approves them as utility, and does your platform support a URL button and a PDF header in a utility template?
  7. ImportantAny limits or rules on sending the SAME notice on WhatsApp and SMS at the same moment? Any de-duplication on your side we must turn off?
  8. BlockerPricing of utility vs authentication messages, and whether a utility message sent inside an open 24-hour window is free.

  10. ImportantEligibility per template: can we tag templates and have your platform enforce who may receive them (for example utility templates only to registered, opted-in parents and students), or do we enforce this on our side only?
