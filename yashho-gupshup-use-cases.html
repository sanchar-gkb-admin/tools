YASHHO × Gupshupmessaging use cases

How YASHHO plans to use Gupshup for WhatsApp and SMS from launch, with RCS as a candidate after launch. Pilot and beta use email only. Every name, number and order id below is a fake sample.

The scan: the ASLI64™ Master Success Scan (Rs 4,999 + 18% GST) is an at-home, online, expert-guided, parent-involved programme: 21 structured tasks (about 12 minutes each, about 5 hours in total) done in the YASHHO app. It measures 64 Essential Success Abilities (AACE64: Attitude, Aptitude, Communication, Execution) and produces a 49-page Personalized Success Path Chart. SCAN → SHOW → STRENGTHEN → SUCCEED. It has no fixed time or place: it must be started within 7 days of payment and is complete at 18 or more of 21 tasks.

Only documented messages are shown. Every screen and row cites its source: a use case in our CRM register (PAR-, SYS-, YSP-, UC- IDs), a design ruling (R-numbers), or the email our platform sends today.

The rule: context decides the channel. No reply needed → email (and WhatsApp for receipts and urgent notices such as a failed payment). Reply needed → WhatsApp with buttons. Parents: WhatsApp → SMS after 15 min → email, all configurable on our side. Quiet hours 21:00–09:00 except urgent. Opt-out always wins. No child details on WhatsApp, SMS or RCS. Team messages stay in our app.

Our CRM keeps one thread per family. Every message we send is one delivery record with the vendor's message id (R67.5); its delivery status, any SMS fallback and every parent reply must land in that same thread, and in the partner's Needs my reply. Pilot and beta are email only — the WhatsApp composer appears from launch (MSG-07). Each step lists what we need from Gupshup and the question it links to.
