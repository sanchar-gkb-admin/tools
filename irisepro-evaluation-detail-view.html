The Detailed Evaluation View

The actual screen — one student, every task, the submission, the brief it was marked against, and all 37 ability scores. Drawn with real data from unit 519.

Correcting myself: this is not the Yashodarshi panel

I previously said “do not build this, reuse evaluate-detail.blade.php”. That was wrong, and the reason is in the code: the panel renders $student->blindView() — pseudonym, age, class, board. No name. No email. Evaluators mark blind, deliberately.

An admin resolving a parent complaint needs the opposite: the named child, the whole history, and the timings. The marking panel is one pane inside this view — reached from it, showing exactly what the evaluator saw — not a substitute for it.

The fourth tab is where the panel reuse belongs — embedded, read-only, showing the blind view the evaluator actually worked from. That is the honest way to settle “did they have enough to go on?”, and it keeps the audit anchored to the real screen rather than a reconstruction of it.

Same task rail, same record, same abilities — with three differences:

  1. Name and class are shown — changed 7 Sep. Guardian email and phone stay admin-only.
  2. Scoped to mapped units. units.yashodarshi_id, the existing guard, unchanged.
  3. Read-only, plus one action: add feedback — the gap this student's record actually has.

⭐ Blind marking is being dropped — Goutam & KMM sir, 7 Sep

The evaluator now sees the child's name and class. The reason is operational and it is a good one: a pseudonym makes it genuinely hard to track whose evaluation is whose. This escalation opened with “Candidate: 8E939” and it took real work to establish that meant A Varshikaa — friction paid on every single query.

What changes in code: Student::blindView() already returns class, board and age; only the name is missing. It is called from five places, so adding it once updates every evaluator surface consistently.

What must NOT come with it: guardian email and phone. An evaluator has no operational need for either, and it is a child's contact data. Identity solves the tracking problem; contact details do not, and they carry a DPDPA minimisation cost for nothing.

⛔ Two comments become false the moment this ships and must be updated in the same change, or the next reader will trust the wrong one: EvaluatorDesk.php:19 — “BLIND — a candidate is ONLY Student::blindView(); name/email/phone never leave the model [#80]” — and YashodarshiController.php:59, which describes that desk as the thing this screen must never become a way around. The honest trade is worth recording too: blind marking was an integrity control, and once an evaluator can see who they are marking, an overturn after a complaint is no longer a review of the same conditions. That is an accepted cost, not an oversight.
