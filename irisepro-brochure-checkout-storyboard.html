The form is the same form. Fields are exactly today's — First name, Last name, Email, Mobile. Only the heading, the amount and the "what's included" block are driven by the product.
The STREAM SELECTOR DISAPPEARS. Today the form asks which stream, because a plan is a tier and a tier is stream-agnostic — the buyer has to re-state something the page they came from already knew. A product key carries its stream, so the field becomes a confirmation, not a question. One fewer thing to get wrong, and it removes a real mismatch: nothing today stops someone picking KAS on a NEET card.
A bundle shows its legs. 3A/3B are packaging over existing products, so the form lists what the buyer is actually getting rather than a name they must decode.
The URL carries a PRODUCT, not a price. ?product=kas_2a replaces ?plan=<slug>. A price in a URL is tamperable and a tier is not an identity — the same defect that let a ₹5,999 learner be treated as a ₹4,999 one.

The plan pages are the SEAM. Brochure, new site, an ad, a WhatsApp share — all point at one URL per product. Whatever the front end becomes, the purchase path underneath does not move again.
Which is why the new site must NOT get its own checkout. A second buy flow built for the new IA would be a second payments authority, and it would diverge from the brochure's the first time a price or a readiness rule changed. The IA storyboard's single CTA rail routes here rather than replacing it.

Why one form and not two. A scan form plus a "booster form" would mean two places that mint an order, two that resolve a price, and two to keep in step with the catalogue. The single-authority rule exists because that divergence is silent — it shows up as a learner charged the wrong amount, months later.
The blocker is not the form — it is what a purchase IS. Today a purchase is recorded as payments.challenge_id, and there is no challenge for 2A–3B. The form can be upgraded in a day; the money path has to be able to say "this person bought kas_2a" before that day is worth anything. payment_grants already has exactly that shape and has never been written in production.
