KAS MCQ — performance & scorecard

Marks, areas, behaviour, abilities and pillars — across paper, set and the whole journey. Five kinds of information at three zoom levels is fifteen views if you're careless. This is three.

1 · The structure

One screen, three zoom levels, five rungs. The rungs never change order, so the student learns the shape once and re-reads it at each level.

1
How did I do?Marks, with the derivation and a cutoff reference. Concrete. The number they came for.
2
Where did I lose it?By subject area, sorted by marks lost — not alphabetically, and not all sixteen. Still concrete. This is what they revise from.
3
What do my answers say?Timing and attempt pattern, read back as plain observations before any score. The bridge. Behaviour is observable, so it earns the interpretation that follows.
4
What does that make me good at?Four pillars, each opening to its abilities, against the benchmarks. Interpretive — and only credible because rungs 1–3 came first.
5
Am I getting better?Movement per ability across the sets. Journey level only. The reason to buy eight sets rather than one.
Why this order and not the reverse. The instinct is to lead with the ability profile — it is the differentiated thing we sell. But an ability claim with no visible evidence under it reads as an assertion, and a candidate who cannot see where they lost marks will not believe a number telling them their Comprehension is below benchmark. Each rung earns the next. Marks are checkable, areas are checkable, timing is checkable — by the time the abilities appear the student has already agreed with three screens' worth of facts.
Overall AACE uses the engine's existing convention, unchanged. scorecard-engine/src/cli.py:155 — overall_total / (overall_count × 5) × 100. That is already what "Overall AACE" means on both the card and the in-app dashboard, so MCQ inherits it rather than defining a new one.

It is the marks in percentage form — with weights summing to 100 per slot the weighting cancels exactly, so it equals percent-correct. That is the point of showing it: 230.25 / 400 cannot be set against a benchmark of 92, and 57.6% can. Two numbers that are the same thing in different units is a scale conversion; two that disagree would be a support ticket.

Pillars use the same convention scoped to their own abilities (cli.py:151-153). The spread across the fifteen is the diagnostic signal — their mean is not, which is why the abilities get rung 4 and the headline gets one line.
Never-reached questions follow the engine's [#101] achievement-vs-diagnostic split, already locked 9 Jul. In cli.py:135-139 a not-attempted instance scores 0 but stays in the denominator for Overall, and drops out of both sides for pillars and abilities. Applied one level down: a paper that expired at Q64 costs the student marks on the 36 they never saw, and says nothing about their Comprehension. Same rule, no new one.

Communication is not shown — only one of the fifteen abilities sits in it, so a pillar score would just restate that one number.
Two decisions inside rung 4. Pillars are the summary and abilities the detail, collapsed by default — four things to read instead of fifteen, and it matches the existing AACE Breakdown pattern. And the Communication pillar does not render. Mapping the KAS Top 15 onto the framework's four pillars gives Execution 7, Aptitude 4, Attitude 3, and Communication exactly one — Structure. A pillar score computed from a single ability is that ability wearing a hat, and if Structure is thinly weighted the engine computes the pillar as 0.0 and the card tells a candidate their communication is "Weak" on the basis of nothing.

Rung 5 is where the monthly plan justifies itself — and the sparklines do more work than the numbers. A student reading "Attention to Detail 81" learns nothing they can act on; one seeing three rising bars and one falling learns exactly where to put the next hour. The falling one is deliberately not hidden.

"If you change one thing" is the only place the card gives an instruction, and it is assembled from the rungs above rather than generated — the rush is rung 3, the blanks are rung 3, the 79% is rung 1. Nothing new is asserted.
