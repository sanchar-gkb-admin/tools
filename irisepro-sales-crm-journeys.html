One family.One page.Every role.

This storyboard shows how the YASHHO sales network will work in one system, screen by screen. It is for Goutam to review and sign off before the technical design starts.

Our customer is always a family: a parent and their child. Every family has one page. Everyone who works with that family opens the same page, and sees only what their job needs.

Families come in two waysDirectly, through a YSP, an RSM or the website, when the parent buys the ₹4,999 scan. Or through their school or college, which is our partner, not our customer.

Who gets credit for a sale At launch

Every paid sale is credited once, at the moment of payment, by one rule. A sale belongs to a YSP or RSM only if the parent was already one of their families, or came through their own link or QR code. Everything else is a YASHHO direct sale, and nobody earns commission on it.

1The rule

Three questions, asked in this order the moment the payment comes in. A family's partner always comes before anyone else's link. The first "yes" decides, and the answer is saved with the sale for good.

YASHHO CRM · How a sale is credited
1 · Is the parent already one of a partner's families?

Matched by the family's payment link, mobile number or email, saved before the payment. Yes → that YSP or RSM.

Flows A and B
2 · Did they come through a partner's own link or QR?

Within 90 days of the click. Yes → that YSP or RSM, and the family becomes theirs.

See D
3 · Neither?

A YASHHO direct sale. No YSP or RSM gets credit or commission.

Flow C
Why it can never be two people

A mobile number or email can belong to only one family, and a family to only one partner. The first person to save the family keeps it. Only the GM can move a family, and every move is logged.

The PIN code is used to show sales by region. It never decides who gets a sale.
