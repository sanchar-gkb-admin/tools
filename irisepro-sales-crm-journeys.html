YASHHO CRM · Storyboard

How every person in the YASHHO sales network will use one system, screen by screen. This version is for Goutam to review and sign off before the technical design starts.

The big idea in one line: our customer is always a family: a parent and their child. Every family has one page in the system. Everyone who works with that family opens the same page. What each person can see and do on it depends on their job.

What we build first, for launch: everything a YSP and an RSM need, plus what the GM, CEO, Field Sales manager and Finance need to approve and keep an eye on things.
What comes later: screens for the Head Office teams (online counsellors, Ambassadors, webinars, TV enquiries). They will use the same family pages, so nothing has to be rebuilt.

0 · One family, one page

Our customer is always a family: a parent and their child. Everything we know about them lives on one page, whoever brought them in.

Two ways a family comes to us
1. Directly. A YSP or RSM meets the parent, or the parent finds us on the website. The parent pays ₹4,999 for the scan.
2. Through their school or college. The YSP signs up a school or college for the school programme. Its students become families on our system too, linked to that school. The programme fee is collected through the school.
A school or college is not the customer. It is a partner that brings us families. It has its own page listing its students, and each student also has their family page.
Who brings families in
Directly: YSPs in their area · RSMs in their own ward and friends circle · the website and Facebook/Instagram ads · later: Ambassadors, webinars, TV
Through a school: the school or college the YSP signs up
Who works with them
The family's YSP, guided by the RSM. Leaders watch the numbers.

The family's page

👪 Parent and child

🚪 How they came: directly, or through their school

📍 Where they live → which YSP and RSM look after them

🤝 Who met them first (that person keeps them)

✅ Permission to contact them

🕑 Every call, visit, message and enquiry

💳 Payments and the scan report delivered

What it feeds
Targets and progress for each YSP, Region and the whole state
What it pays
Commission once the scan report is delivered, worked out on the amount after GST
