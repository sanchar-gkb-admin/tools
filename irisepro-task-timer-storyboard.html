Task Timer — UX Storyboard

The per-task clock, redrawn so it's obvious the moment the task opens (the clock starts on view — reading time counts, by design), impossible to lose while you work, escalating at 50 / 85 / 95%, and — when time runs out — it says so plainly and moves you to the next task instead of stalling. Submissions are written, audio, or video uploads; every screen keeps a "Choose from device" path. Follow the arrows.

Keeps flowing: lands straight on Task 10 — its clock starts on open too, stated up front.
One clock, everywhere. The pinned timer and every banner read the single server-side expires_at (re-synced via the existing timer-status route), so the number is identical on the task and submission screens and can't be gamed by a refresh or a skewed device clock.

Thresholds fire once each at 50 / 85 / 95% of the task's own duration — a 25-min task and a 90-min sprint escalate on the same rhythm without hard-coded minutes. Upload always available: every task offers "Choose from device" (plus Record / Take-photo where relevant) — never record-only. Auto-save at 0:00 (Phase 2) finalizes a fully-uploaded submission; nothing uploaded = not attempted. Accessibility: the countdown is an aria-live region and the pulse is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
