From the merged spec 2026-11-24-learner-placement-and-stream-change-design.md (§5, §5.1a–c, §5.2). Same enrolment row, payment untouched, old stream superseded and voided, learner lands on a clean slate. Worked example: the 23-Nov case — Marks Booster ₹4,999 → JEE ₹4,999, batch 1888, Yashodarshi Sutirtha (YD). Learner shown as a Candidate code.

06 · Approval queue (supervisor) →

Approve re-runs the preview; if anything changed since (stale), it refuses and asks for a fresh preview. Reject needs a reason.
