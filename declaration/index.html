Every day, from scratch

A public declaration. I am a cause in the matter of my life — not its author, not its victim, its cause. What follows is not what I hope for. It is what I am doing.

I

Scan

the body first — nothing above this is possible without it

Every day

Ninety minutes on foot

I walk ninety minutes a day. I get sunshine every day — not as exercise, as intake.

Every day

Time under trees

I spend time in nature. I breathe. I meditate. The nervous system is given somewhere to go.

Always

Mindfulness in everything

I practise mindfulness in the doing — in the code, the call, the queue, the conversation. Not a session at dawn and a different man by noon.

Attention is the only instrument I have. Everything else is downstream of how I keep it.

II

Recognise

three paths, running together

Madhumita

Her own institution

Built to her standard, on her subject, not folded into mine. Hers to describe when she chooses to.

My son

His own craft

Creative work on genuinely hard problems — the kind of practice you are reached for when the problem is difficult, not when it needs decorating.

Mine

YASHHO, and the science under it

Human development taken seriously enough to be researched, taught and measured — so a child is seen clearly instead of sorted early.

Her school. His craft. My platform.
Not one of us chose safe.

III

Activate

the study, and the discipline that makes study possible

The research

The Technology of Human Development

I study what is actually involved in the development and evolution of the human species — biology, zoology, sociology, psychology, neuroscience. Not as background reading. As the discipline the whole venture rests on.

One book. Every day.

Stop

Worrying

I have stopped worrying. Action, action, action — never procrastination. Worry is the tax on a decision not yet made.

Start

Scheduling like it matters

I schedule my time as though everything in my life matters, because it does. A calendar is the only honest record of what a man actually believes.

Daily

Restore integrity

I restore my integrity every day, starting from scratch. Not a state reached once, but a thing rebuilt each morning by whoever I was yesterday.

The engine

A flywheel, not a funnel

I am building the platform that delivers an unmatched user experience. Best-in-class NPS — measured on retention and on growth through organic referral. The flywheel turns. Paid acquisition is what you do when the product is not yet good enough to be talked about.

Bangalore – Mysore

A world-class R&D centre on Human Excellence

I am building it: human excellence and curriculum development, researched properly and openly. An institution that makes our communities and our nation proud, and a global organisation with its feet in the Bangalore–Mysore corridor rather than borrowed from elsewhere.

Every year

One journey out

I travel out once a year. A new language, a new culture, every year. The map means nothing unless you have stood on some of it.

The land

A house that feeds itself

A five-bedroom villa, self-sufficient in its own energy. Organic farming. Farm-fresh and local on the table. The community around it self-sustaining in what it consumes and what it burns.

The keeping

Air, water, soil

I protect the air, the water and the soil around me. Three things no revenue line replaces and no apology restores.

And

The car

A Porsche. Or a BMW. Written down rather than left off to look serious — a thing wanted is still a thing wanted.

Nothing about this is easy, and it was never going to be.

Which is why integrity is not a state I reach — it is a thing I rebuild every morning, from scratch.
