Immutability #34 · issue #47 · 12 Sep 2026

When a scorecard is revised,
what does its reader see?

A re-mark now appends a new version and keeps the old one. The guardian gets a quiet in-app notice (#46, decided: in-app only, names the task, never the number). This is the other half — the card itself. Three options, rendered on the real cover.

01 The notice they get first decided · #46

In-app only. No email. It names the task and never the number or the direction.

This is the option that can hurt. Everything else in this workstream — "names the task, never the number", "identical wording in both directions" — exists to stop a child reading their worth as a delta. A link to the old edition undoes that in one tap.

Recommendation — Option A, and file B

Option A is the only one that is already true: the cover date moves on its own because the appended version carries a fresh generated_at. It costs nothing, ships with the code release, and adds no new question a reader cannot answer.

Why not B now: the honest case for it is a parent who notices a different number and finds no record. But they do get a record — the in-app notice from #46, which names the task. B is the right follow-up if support ever fields “why is this different”; building it first spends the six-step engine tail on a problem we have not yet seen.
What none of these do: tell a Yashodarshi that a task they marked was re-marked. The marker-side surface is not in #47 and is not in this release — worth its own issue before send-back is ever switched on.
