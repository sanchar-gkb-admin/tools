Items marked proposal need backend work that is not built yet. People, post codes and amounts are samples.

Money is shown in ₹ with Indian grouping. Paise rule (N15): every line, TDS and total is stored in paise (TDS floored to the paisa, never to the rupee); a figure is drawn with its paise (₹1,234.50) whenever the paise are not zero, and the CA export always carries two decimals, so every row adds up to the total shown. The samples below use whole rupees only because every sample amount has zero paise. A test asserts that the sum of the stored lines equals each displayed total. Retention (N16): payout lines, TDS rows and ledger rows are append-only tax records, kept for the statutory period and excluded from a partner's DPDP erasure until it ends (partial erasure of the person's other data; the length of the period is a CA question). Commission is earned on the amount before GST (memory: decision-one-price-all-channels).
