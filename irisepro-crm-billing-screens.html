GET and POST /crm/billing/payout-runs, POST …/paid (routes/crm.php:660-672), ability payout.manage. Finance proposes from the closed month's statement; the GM or CEO approves on the approvals queue (routes/crm.php:825-833); Finance records each line paid with bank reference and date.

2a refused: TDS rules unset→2b ready to propose→2c approval pending→2d GM/CEO approves→2e approved · mark paid→2f paid sheet→2g all paid

3 · CEO override for an Annual refund

POST /crm/billing/refund-overrides (routes/crm.php:674-678), ability refund_override.propose (Finance or GM). The one exception to the Annual no-refund-after-first-challenge rule: a reason is required, the CEO alone approves, and the override is audited (ProposeAnnualRefundOverride.php:10-16).

3a propose (reason required)→3b CEO approves→3c override in force

3a · Finance or the GM proposes the override, with a reason BILL-F-05

The refund is blocked because the first challenge has started. Proposing needs a reason; without one it is refused.
