Model & sources

auditable

Fixed cost is the live Mumbai (ap-south-1) AWS run-rate — a flat ~$8.25/day since go-live mid-July 2026, pulled from Cost Explorer (daily granularity, tax excluded). The retiring EU (eu-north-1, ~$250/mo) stack is excluded.

Media caps from Backend-laravel/config/tasks.php: video 25 MB / 90 s @ 720p, audio 20 MB cap (~2 MB stored @ AAC 128k), image/PDF 25 MB cap (~1 MB stored). Transcode: infra/transcode-lambda/handler.py (ffmpeg → 1280px H.264 CRF 28). The per-task media mix is your product policy, not a code constant — hence editable.

Storage model (measured + verified 28-Jul-2026): every upload stores both the original and an optimized sibling under task_responses/. A single S3 lifecycle (s3.tf) hard-expires all of it at 90 days — confirmed live on the bucket (oldest object = 14 days), so storage is bounded by this window and does not accumulate over tenure. Objects >2 MB tier to STANDARD_IA at 30 days. Video & audio originals are inline-deleted after evaluation; image originals persist for legibility/appeal. Sizes above are measured across 4,843 real objects (video ≈ 98% of bytes). Egress via CloudFront; uploads are free.

  • Variable/student = storage (both copies, 90d cap) + transcode + egress + SES + scorecard render.
  • Tech COGS/student = variable + (fixed total ÷ active students).
  • Human eval is labour, not infra — separate line, editable ₹/task.
Caveat: August 2026 is the first clean full month of Mumbai-only billing — re-check the fixed lines against that actual before quoting externally.
