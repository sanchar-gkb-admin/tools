A short design exercise

Thanks for getting this far. This is the last step before we talk properly.

We want to see how you work, not how much you can polish. So this is deliberately small, and we'd like you to keep it that way.

The setup

At YASHHO, ideas usually arrive as a conversation. Our founder KMM will explain something he's thinking about, at length, and somewhere in there is the thing we need to build. Turning that into something a developer can work from is most of the job.

So that's what we're asking you to do.

What we're sending you

A voice note from KMM, sent separately with this brief. It's about three minutes and we haven't edited it. It rambles a bit, which is honest, because that's how it actually reaches us.

A link to the page it relates to, also sent with the brief.

What we'd like back

1

A short storyboard

A sequence of screens showing what someone would see and do, on a phone and on a desktop. Rough is completely fine. Hand-drawn on paper and photographed is completely fine. We care about the thinking, not the finish.

2

A Loom walking us through it

Five to eight minutes, screen shared, covering:

  • What you took KMM to mean, especially anywhere you had to interpret
  • What you decided, and what you were unsure about
  • Anything you'd have asked him if you could
  • Anything you deliberately left out, and why
  • How you used AI, if you did. We use it heavily and we're genuinely interested in how you work with it

One take is fine. Please don't re-record it. We're not assessing polish, and a slightly messy first take tells us more than a rehearsed one.

Time

Ninety minutes for the work, plus however long the Loom takes you.

We know an exercise like this can quietly eat a weekend, and we don't want that. If ninety minutes isn't enough to finish, send what you have and say what you'd have done next. That's a perfectly good answer and we'll read it as one.

If you find yourself polishing, stop. Rough work with clear thinking beats a beautiful screen you can't explain.

What we do with it

Nothing. This is for the conversation and nothing else. We won't use your work on the site, and we'll delete it once we've made a decision.

Whatever we decide, we'll give you our honest read on what you sent. That seems the least we can do for your time.

What happens next

We watch the Loom first. If it's a fit, we'll set up a proper conversation with the core team, in person if you're in Bengaluru or over Zoom if not. That's a conversation, not a presentation, and you won't need to prepare anything new for it.

Two things worth saying

You can ask us questions before you start. Please do. Just reply to the email. Knowing which questions you ask is useful to us, and it's how the job actually works.

We're not looking for a redesign of our site. If your answer to the voice note is one striking screen with nothing around it, that's not what we need. We need someone who can show a whole journey, including the boring states, and who can explain their choices.
