Auto-detect

Land on the right language

A visitor hitting bare /kas is sent to /kas/kn if their browser prefers Kannada, else /kas/en. (Ad links to /kas/en or /kas/kn still win.)

Manual switch

EN | ಕನ್ನಡ toggle

The pill in the top bar switches instantly and updates the URL, so the choice is shareable and bookmarkable.

Remembered

Sticks on return

Their pick is remembered, so a return visit to /kas opens the language they last chose — over the browser default.

One decision for you: auto-detect reads the browser's Accept-Language. That needs a tiny redirect step on /kas (today it hard-sends everyone to /kas/en). Fine to add? — my recommendation: yes.
